Outbound teams usually pick a CRM after the sending stack is already running. The question is which CRM records replies, books meetings and tracks deals without getting in the way. HubSpot and Pipedrive are two of the most common answers, and they are built differently.

We have compared Close vs HubSpot and Close vs Pipedrive, and ranked four CRMs in our best CRM for B2B outbound roundup. This is the direct HubSpot vs Pipedrive comparison.

Sourcing note: HubSpot details come from its Sales Hub pricing page, read in October 2026. Pipedrive's pricing page returned a 403 error, so Pipedrive plan prices come from third-party sources and are marked (directional); plan limits come from Pipedrive's help center. Confirm all prices on the live pages before buying.

The Short Answer

Choose HubSpot if you want one platform for outbound and inbound, deeper reporting, and ready-made connectors to your LinkedIn and email tools, and you can absorb the Professional-tier cost. Choose Pipedrive if you are a small team that wants a simple visual deal pipeline, accepts lighter reporting, and will connect outbound tools through Zapier or webhooks. If you mostly sell by phone and email to a defined list, also look at Close.

Pricing and What Each Plan Unlocks

HubSpot Sales Hub

HubSpot's page lists Starter at $7 and $20 per seat, Professional at $90 and $100, and Enterprise at $150, without labelling monthly versus annual; our Sales Hub pricing guide reads the lower figure as annual billing. Professional carries a required $1,500 onboarding fee and Enterprise $3,500. View-only seats are free. Included HubSpot Credits rise from 500 on Starter to 3,000 and 5,000 on the higher tiers.

The automation limits are where outbound teams should read carefully. The live page lists up to 50 workflows on Starter (with restricted triggers and actions), up to 300 on Professional and up to 1,000 on Enterprise, and it places sequence-triggered workflows under Enterprise. If your plan depends on enrolling people in sequences automatically, confirm which tier you need on HubSpot's current page. For the mechanics, see HubSpot workflow automation for reply handling.

Pipedrive

Pipedrive renamed its plans in 2025: Essential became Lite, Advanced became Growth, Professional and Power merged into Premium, and Enterprise became Ultimate. A competitor's pricing roundup from December 2025 lists annual prices of about €14, €39, €49 and €79 per seat, and monthly prices of €24, €49, €79 and €99 (directional). Pipedrive prices regionally and in several currencies, so treat these as a guide only.

From Pipedrive's own help center, the limits that matter most for outbound are these: Premium allows 25 sequences, 150 active automations and 10 if/else steps per automation; lead scoring is Premium and above; forecasting reports start on Growth; and lead and deal limits are 2,500 per user on Lite, 5,000 on Growth and 15,000 per company on Premium. Several cells in the help table were blank for the other tiers, so we do not state those.

Add-ons change the comparison. Pipedrive sells Campaigns, LeadBooster, Web Visitors and Smart Docs separately, at roughly €13 to €41 and up per month each (directional). HubSpot's higher tiers bundle more. Price the exact features you need on each side, not just the headline seat.

The Data Model: Why It Matters for Outbound

Outbound creates a lot of people and few deals. You might contact 4,000 prospects and have 60 live opportunities. HubSpot's contact and company objects are designed for that shape: you can hold every prospect, record every touch, and create a deal only when someone engages. Pipedrive is deal-centric, and its Sequences enrol deals rather than contacts. That works for a small, high-touch pipeline but gets awkward at outbound volumes. Our Close vs Pipedrive post goes deeper on this.

On the HubSpot side, define your pipeline carefully so replies turn into deals cleanly: see HubSpot deal stages for outbound teams. If you need custom objects for things like accounts or campaigns, they are an Enterprise feature (see HubSpot custom objects). We did not verify whether Pipedrive offers an equivalent.

Cold Email and LinkedIn Integrations

This is where the two CRMs differ most for a team doing cold email and LinkedIn outreach.

  • HeyReach with HubSpot: a native app syncs LinkedIn activity as notes, timeline events and properties. It only matches existing contacts by email and does not create new ones, and it needs HeyReach's Growth plan or above. See our HeyReach HubSpot guide.
  • Instantly with HubSpot: Instantly's marketplace page describes native import of contacts and lists. Sending outcomes back to HubSpot goes through connectors or webhook tools such as OutboundSync, and a HubSpot community thread says native reverse sync is not supported (directional). See Instantly HubSpot integration.
  • Pipedrive: we found no official native page for HeyReach or Instantly. Zapier lists connectors for both (directional), and both tools offer webhooks or an API. Smartlead and Clay support for either CRM we did not verify; check each vendor's docs.

In practice, a HubSpot setup gets you further with less glue, while a Pipedrive setup usually means building a Zapier or webhook layer you maintain yourself.

Sequences Are Not a Cold Email Engine

Neither CRM replaces a sending platform. HubSpot caps sequence sends at 500 or 1,000 per user per day, and neither tool is built around rotating many mailboxes across domains. Use the CRM for pipeline and reporting, and a dedicated sender for volume. We unpack that split in HubSpot sequences vs cold email tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper for an outbound team, HubSpot or Pipedrive?

Pipedrive is cheaper per seat. HubSpot's Professional tier costs more and adds a $1,500 onboarding fee, though it bundles more features. Pipedrive's current prices could not be read on its pricing page, so confirm them there.

Do I need HubSpot Professional or Enterprise for outbound automation?

It depends on what you automate. Starter has a small, restricted workflow allowance. The live pricing page lists sequence-triggered workflows under Enterprise, so check the current tier before committing to an automation-heavy design.

Does Pipedrive have a built-in dialer?

No. Pipedrive's help center points to Marketplace calling apps. HubSpot includes calling minutes on each Sales Hub tier, rising from 500 on Starter to 12,000 on Enterprise.

Can either CRM replace Smartlead or Instantly?

Not at real volume. HubSpot sequences cap at 500 or 1,000 emails per user per day, and neither CRM is built for multi-mailbox, multi-domain sending. Use the CRM as the system of record and a dedicated tool for sending.

Which integrates better with HeyReach and Instantly?

HubSpot, because both tools publish HubSpot connectors, with caveats about contact matching and reverse sync. For Pipedrive we found Zapier, webhook or API routes only (directional).

Does Pipedrive's deal-centric model hurt outbound?

It can. Sequences enrol deals, so a team that contacts thousands of prospects but holds few deals ends up creating deals early or working around the model. HubSpot's contact and company objects handle large prospect lists more naturally.
