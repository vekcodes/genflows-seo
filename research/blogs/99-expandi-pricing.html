Expandi is a cloud-based LinkedIn automation tool, and its pricing is refreshingly simple next to most outreach software: one plan, one per-seat price, and an agency tier you have to talk to sales about. The catch is that a flat per-seat price can hide the real cost of running LinkedIn outreach at scale, because every extra profile is another seat. This guide lays out the plans, what a seat is, the trial, refund and renewal terms, and a worked total for one, three and ten accounts.

The Short Answer

Expandi costs $99 per LinkedIn account per month, or $79 per account per month if you pay annually. A solo founder running one profile pays $948 a year on annual billing. An agency running ten profiles is looking at roughly $9,480 a year at list price, and Expandi says 10+ seats move to custom pricing, so ask for a quote before assuming list price. Because the trial is only 7 days and fees are nonrefundable, test one seat properly before you commit to a year.

Expandi describes the annual option as a 20% discount, equivalent to two months free. Taxes can be added depending on your country.

What a "Seat" Means

A seat is one connected LinkedIn account. The pricing page states the price as "per seat/LinkedIn account", and the Terms describe adding or removing a seat under an existing subscription as a prorated amount added to your next invoice. So a second profile means a second seat, whether it belongs to a colleague or to you. The Terms also state that only personal LinkedIn accounts can be connected.

That is the same meter HeyReach and most LinkedIn tools use, and it is the opposite of cold email, where cost follows sending volume. We cover the difference in our cold email vs LinkedIn outreach comparison.

What the Business Plan Includes

There are no feature tiers. The Business plan lists cloud-based operation, a dedicated country-based IP, profile warm-up, unlimited campaigns, multichannel outreach (connection requests, messages and email), campaign prioritization, templates, scheduling, a blacklist and integrations. Expandi also lists campaign types that work around some LinkedIn limits.

Not on the pricing page: image and GIF personalization costs extra, and video personalization is a paid add-on through a third party. We could not find a published price for either, so budget for them as unknowns if personalized visuals are part of your plan.

What Agency Pricing Adds

For 10+ seats, Expandi's pricing page says "the more accounts you take the more you save" and points you to sales. The Agency tier includes everything in Business plus flexible roles and permission management, centralized campaign management, client reporting, template sharing, specialized support, a white-label option and a dedicated customer success manager. No per-seat Agency price or discount schedule is published, so any figure you see elsewhere is a guess.

The Cost Per Account Is Not the Cost Per Meeting

A seat is only useful if the account stays alive and sends enough to matter. LinkedIn caps how many connection requests an account can send, and the commonly cited ceiling is around 100 invitations a week per profile (directional), so a single $79 to $99 seat produces a bounded amount of outreach whatever tool you use. Scaling volume means more accounts, which means more seats. Read our LinkedIn outreach limits and account safety guide before you plan volume, and keep the account restriction recovery playbook handy, because a restricted profile is a paid seat producing nothing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Expandi cost in 2026?

The Business plan is $99 per month per LinkedIn account on monthly billing, or $79 per month per account on annual billing. For 10+ seats, Expandi offers custom Agency pricing. Taxes may apply depending on your country.

Does Expandi have a free trial?

Yes, a 7-day free trial with access to all features and campaign types. The Terms say the trial applies only to the first subscription on a user account, and a second subscription is charged from the date it is activated.

Can I get a refund on Expandi?

Generally no. The Terms say subscription fees are nonrefundable unless required by law. Because the subscription auto-renews for the same period you chose, cancel before renewal if you do not want another term, and use the trial to decide before paying.

What is a seat in Expandi?

A seat is one connected LinkedIn account. A second profile needs a second seat, and adding or removing a seat is prorated onto your next invoice. Only personal LinkedIn accounts can be connected.

Does Expandi offer agency or white-label pricing?

Yes, for 10+ seats. The Agency tier adds roles and permissions, centralized campaign management, client reporting, template sharing, specialized support, a white-label option and a dedicated customer success manager. The price is custom and not published, so you need to request a quote.

Is the annual plan worth it?

It saves 20%, or $240 a year per seat on list prices, but it is a larger commitment with no refunds. Run the 7-day trial and a short monthly period first, then switch to annual once your outreach is producing replies.
