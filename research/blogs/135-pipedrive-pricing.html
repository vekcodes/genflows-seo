Pipedrive is one of the most common CRMs for small sales teams, and it is a frequent choice when an outbound team wants something lighter than HubSpot. Its pricing changed in a 2025 restructure, so older articles list plan names that no longer exist. This guide covers the current plans, the limits that matter for outbound, and what a team really pays once a cold email and LinkedIn stack is attached.

The Short Answer

Pipedrive has four plans: Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. On annual billing the per-seat prices are roughly $14, $39, $59 and $79 per month (directional). For outbound, Growth is the practical floor because sequences, workflow automation and email sync start there. Pipedrive is a good system of record for deals, but it is not a cold email sender, so plan for a dedicated sending tool and a LinkedIn tool next to it.

What Each Plan Means for Outbound

Lite: a contact list, not an outbound tool

Lite has no sequences, no workflow automation and no email sync in the limits we read. It suits a founder tracking a handful of deals, not a team running campaigns.

Growth: the realistic starting point

Growth adds sequences (5 per company), 50 automations, one synced email account per user, forecasting and a contacts timeline. Five sequences covers a basic follow-up process, but a team that runs several segments will hit the cap quickly.

Premium and Ultimate: more headroom

Premium raises sequences to 25 and automations to 150, lets each user sync three mailboxes, and adds a shared team inbox. Ultimate takes sequences to 50 and email accounts to five per user. Premium and Ultimate also include LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs, which Lite and Growth pay for separately.

Seat rule: each plan has a per-seat base price, and you pay for seats whether or not they are assigned to a user. Annual plans are paid upfront for the year.

Add-Ons and Usage Limits

Pipedrive sells several add-ons per company. Campaigns and Web Visitors are paid on every plan; LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs are free on Premium and Ultimate only. Starting prices from search summaries were about $13.33 per month for Campaigns, $32.50 for LeadBooster and $41 for Web Visitors on annual billing (directional). Top-ups exist for API tokens, leads and deals, reports and automations.

  • Contacts: no usage limit.
  • Leads plus deals: scale per seat, capped at 300,000 per company.
  • Automation paths: up to 10 actions and 10 delay steps per path, with a 7-day limit on wait-for-condition steps.

Watch the per-seat limits: leads, deals and API tokens are multiplied by seat count, so a team that imports large cold lists into the CRM can approach the cap faster than the headline number suggests. Keep raw prospect lists in your enrichment tool and push only engaged contacts into Pipedrive.

Can You Run Cold Outbound From Pipedrive?

Not at volume. Pipedrive's own support pages say workflow automation is meant for one-off emails, such as a deal changing stage, and that sending follows your connected mailbox's limits. For bulk sending it points to group emailing or the Campaigns add-on, and Campaigns only sends to contacts with a subscribed marketing status and requires an unsubscribe footer. That is a marketing-email model, not a cold outreach one.

We did not find anything confirming inbox rotation, warm-up or deliverability monitoring in Pipedrive sequences, or LinkedIn steps. In practice, teams pair it with a dedicated sender. Who Pipedrive Fits

Good fit: small teams that want a simple visual pipeline, a low seat price and an outbound stack built from specialised tools around it.

Weaker fit: teams that want sequencing, calling and reporting in one outbound-first CRM, or that need heavy RevOps automation such as custom objects and complex lifecycle logic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Pipedrive's plans in 2026?

Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. A 2025 restructure replaced the older Essential, Advanced, Professional/Power and Enterprise names, so older articles may list plans that no longer exist.

How much does Pipedrive cost per user?

On annual billing, roughly $14 for Lite, $39 for Growth, $59 for Premium and $79 for Ultimate per seat per month (directional, from search summaries). Monthly billing is more expensive. Check the live pricing page for current figures.

Does Pipedrive have native sequences?

Yes, from Growth. The limit is 5 sequences per company on Growth, 25 on Premium and 50 on Ultimate. Lite has none.

Can I send cold email in bulk from Pipedrive?

Not as a primary tool. Pipedrive says automation is not a mass-email tool, and Campaigns sends only to contacts who are subscribed. Most outbound teams use a dedicated sender and sync replies back; see our cold email software comparison.

Which add-ons are included on higher plans?

LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs are included on Premium and Ultimate and paid on Lite and Growth. Campaigns and Web Visitors cost extra on every plan.

Are there limits on contacts or API calls?

Contacts are unlimited. Leads and deals scale per seat up to 300,000 per company, and API tokens scale per seat up to 100 million per company on every plan.
