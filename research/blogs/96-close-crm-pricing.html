TL;DR

  • Four plans, priced per user: Solo $19 monthly or $9 annual, Essentials $49 or $35, Growth $109 or $99, Scale $149 or $139.
  • The plan decides your outbound tooling. Workflows, bulk email and the power dialer start on Growth. The predictive dialer, custom objects and role-based access are Scale only.
  • Calling and SMS are billed on top at usage rates, and US texting needs carrier registration. The seat price is not the whole bill.
  • Monthly billing costs the most on Essentials (about 40% more per seat than annual) and the least on Scale (about 7% more).
  • Trial and guarantee: 14 days free with no credit card, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee on the pricing page.

Close is a CRM built for teams that sell by phone and email, which makes its pricing different from a general-purpose CRM. The per-seat price is straightforward. What is harder to work out is which plan an outbound team actually needs, and what calling, texting and AI usage add on top. This guide covers every plan, what is locked to each tier, the usage costs, and what 3-, 10- and 25-seat teams really pay.

The Short Answer

Most outbound teams that dial and run email sequences land on Growth: $99 per user per month billed annually, or $109 billed monthly. It is the first plan with workflows, bulk email and the power dialer. Essentials suits small teams that only need a shared inbox and manual calling. Scale is for teams that need the predictive dialer, custom objects or role-based permissions. A 10-seat Growth team pays $990 a month on annual billing before calling and SMS usage.

API access is on every plan, and calling and SMS are available on every plan on a usage basis. Custom fields are capped at 250 across the board.

Essentials is a trap for outbound. It has no bulk email, no workflows and no power dialer, so a team doing real volume outgrows it almost immediately. Growth is the realistic starting point. These are our arithmetic (users times per-user price) and cover seats only. Calling, SMS, numbers, enrichment and add-ons come on top.

As a usage illustration, ten reps each making five hours of calls a month is 3,000 minutes, roughly $60 at the $0.02 rate. That is small next to seat cost, which is why the plan tier matters more than usage for most teams. On Growth, monthly billing costs about 10% more than annual; on Essentials the gap is about 40%, and on Scale about 7%.

Trial and guarantee. Close offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card, and the trial includes $5 of calling and enrichment credit. Trial AI credits (1,000) do not carry over to a paid plan. The pricing page also lists a 30-day money-back guarantee; we could not confirm whether it covers annual plans and first purchases alike, so ask before committing to a year (directional). Monthly plans can be changed or cancelled any time, while annual cancellations take effect at the end of the term.

How Close's Pricing Model Differs From HubSpot

  • Sales-only vs a platform. Close is a single sales-focused product where dialers, workflows and calling live in the plan. HubSpot Sales Hub is one hub in a larger platform, priced by edition and hub. Our HubSpot Sales Hub pricing guide covers that side.
  • Onboarding fees. Close's pricing page lists no onboarding fee. HubSpot's upper tiers carry mandatory one-time onboarding charges, detailed in that guide.
  • Usage billing. Close passes calling and SMS through at usage rates. Compare that against your call volume rather than seat price alone.
  • Marketing and service. Close does not try to be your marketing or service platform. If you need those in one system, the comparison shifts. See our wider CRM roundup for B2B outbound.

Which Plan Should You Pick?

  • One founder testing outbound: Solo on annual billing at $9 is the cheapest way to get calling and email in one place.
  • 2 to 5 reps doing real outbound: Growth. You need workflows, bulk email and the power dialer, and you avoid outgrowing Essentials in a month.
  • 10+ reps, phone-heavy: Scale for the predictive dialer, custom objects and permissions, but only if your dialing volume justifies about $40 more per seat than Growth on annual billing.
  • Thinking about switching CRMs: budget for the move as well as the licence. Our CRM migration checklist and cost-per-meeting budget guide help size that.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Close CRM cost per user?

On annual billing, $9 (Solo), $35 (Essentials), $99 (Growth) or $139 (Scale) per user per month. On monthly billing it is $19, $49, $109 or $149. Calling, SMS, phone numbers and some AI features are billed on top by usage.

Does Close have a free trial?

Yes. It is 14 days with no credit card required, and it includes $5 of calling and enrichment credit. The trial's 1,000 AI credits do not carry over when you move to a paid plan.

Which Close plan includes the power dialer and predictive dialer?

The power dialer starts on Growth. The predictive dialer is available only on Scale. Solo and Essentials have neither, and neither has workflows.

What does calling and texting cost in Close?

Most outbound calls run about $0.02 per minute and phone numbers start at about $1 a month, both varying by country and number type. Texts are billed per 140-character unit at carrier rates, and US SMS needs A2P 10DLC registration, which costs $4 to $44 one-time plus $1.50 to $2 a month.

Can I cancel Close or get a refund?

Monthly plans can be upgraded, downgraded or cancelled at any time. Annual cancellations take effect at the end of the billing term. The pricing page lists a 30-day money-back guarantee, but check its exact scope with Close before buying an annual plan.

Do I need to buy extra AI credits?

Not necessarily. Each plan includes 500 to 2,000 pooled credits per user a month, and Notetaker, chat, AI search and summaries do not use them. Voice agents, enrichment and auto-updates do. Extra credits start at $20 a month for 1,200.
