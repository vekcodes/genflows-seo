TL;DR

  • Three self-serve Outreach plans: Growth $47/mo, Hypergrowth $97/mo and Light Speed $358/mo on monthly billing. Enterprise is custom. Annual billing on Outreach is shown at $37.60, $77.60 and $286.30 a month.
  • Sending accounts are unlimited. Uploaded contacts and monthly emails are not. Growth allows 1,000 contacts and 5,000 emails, Hypergrowth 25,000 contacts and 100,000 to 125,000 emails, Light Speed 100,000 contacts and 500,000 emails.
  • Leads are a separate subscription. Instantly Credits start at $47/mo. The $94, $194 and $555 bundles package Outreach plus Credits.
  • There is no refund. The Terms of Service say all fees are non-refundable, so the free trial is your only free test.
  • Mailboxes are the real bill at scale. On our arithmetic, a 30-mailbox setup spends more on mailboxes than on the Hypergrowth plan.

Instantly's headline price is easy to find, and it is also easy to misread. The platform fee covers sending and warmup, but lead data, mailboxes, domains and a CRM are all sold separately, and the pricing page itself is not fully consistent on one important cap. This guide lays out every plan and limit, the products that cost extra, the trial and no-refund rules, and a worked monthly total for a 10-mailbox and a 30-mailbox program.

The Short Answer

Most small outbound teams land on Hypergrowth at $97 a month, because Growth's 5,000 monthly emails runs out at roughly ten mailboxes. Once you add mailboxes and domains, a 10-mailbox program costs about $153 to $173 a month and a 30-mailbox program about $266 to $326, before lead data. Growth is for testing an offer, Hypergrowth is the working tier for a single company, and Light Speed is for high volume that needs Instantly's server and IP rotation. Budget separately for leads, because the database is not part of Outreach.

Instantly's own pages disagree on Hypergrowth's email cap. The plans-overview help article and the monthly view of the pricing page say 125,000 emails a month. The annual view and the feature-comparison table on the pricing page say 100,000. Older third-party reviews also say 100,000. The Scale bundle on the same page lists 100,000 emails, which adds to the ambiguity.

The annual Outreach figures work out to about 20% off the monthly price (our arithmetic). The bundles are shown at a 10% annual discount, so the saving depends on which product you buy. Two more details matter. The contact cap is a fixed limit across all campaigns, not a per-month allowance, so a list you uploaded last quarter still counts. And the pricing page spells the top plan "Lightspeed" while the help center writes "Light Speed"; they are the same plan.

What separates the tiers

  • Growth to Hypergrowth is a jump from 5,000 to 100,000 or more monthly emails and from 1,000 to 25,000 contacts. Growth is a testing tier. 5,000 emails is about 170 a day, which is a handful of mailboxes at conservative volumes.
  • Hypergrowth to Light Speed adds SISR, which Instantly says assigns dedicated server and IP blocks and swaps out flagged IPs. It is Instantly's own claim, and we have not tested it (directional).
  • Need more than a plan allows? The help center lists an $87/month Outreach add-on that adds 25,000 contacts and 125,000 emails, available on Hypergrowth and Light Speed.
  • Mailboxes are not a tier lever. The pricing page says you can connect and warm as many accounts as you need from any provider. You still pay whoever hosts the mailboxes.

Mailboxes and Domains: The Real Bill

Instantly will sell you the infrastructure, and its help center lists the prices.

  • Domains: $15 per year each.
  • Done-for-you Google account: $5 per month.
  • Done-for-you Airmail account: $4 per month (Airmail is Instantly's own sending infrastructure).
  • Pre-warmed Google account: $10 per month.

Your alternative is buying Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailboxes directly, at about $7 a month for a Google Business Starter seat on our earlier verified figure (check Google's current price; a first-year promotion may apply). Users also report mixed experiences with done-for-you accounts, including domain ownership and setup delays (directional, user-reported), so read the terms before you let a vendor register your domains.

Free Trial, Refunds and Cancellation

  • Free trial: 14 days, no credit card required, capped at 250 contacts and 1,000 emails, plus 100 lead credits.
  • Refunds: none. The Terms of Service (section 5.3) say all fees are non-refundable and non-cancellable and apply this to all subscription plans, Credits and add-on services, including done-for-you and warmup.
  • Auto-renewal: subscriptions renew for the same term at the then-current fee unless you cancel before renewal.
  • Cancelling: the terms say you cancel through account settings and cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period, with no pro rata refund for the unused part.
  • Each product separately: Outreach, Credits, CRM and Inbox Placement are separate subscriptions, so cancelling one does not cancel the others.

The practical lesson is the opposite of a refund-friendly tool: use the 14-day trial to prove deliverability and reply handling, and do not pay annually until a campaign has produced meetings. Compare that with the refund windows in our Smartlead pricing guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Instantly cost in 2026?

Instantly's Outreach plans are $47 a month (Growth), $97 a month (Hypergrowth) and $358 a month (Light Speed) on monthly billing, with Enterprise on custom pricing. Annual billing is shown at $37.60, $77.60 and $286.30 a month. Lead credits, CRM, mailboxes and domains cost extra.

Does Instantly limit the number of email accounts?

No. The pricing page says you can connect and warm as many accounts as you need from any provider. The limits are uploaded contacts and monthly emails. You still pay for each mailbox, from about $4 to $5 a month through Instantly or about $7 a month from Google directly.

Is there an Instantly free trial?

Yes. The trial runs 14 days with no credit card required and is capped at 250 contacts and 1,000 emails, with 100 lead credits. Once you pay, there is no refund window, so use the trial to test deliverability first.

Does Instantly offer refunds?

No. Section 5.3 of the Terms of Service says all fees are non-refundable and non-cancellable, including subscription plans, Credits and add-on services. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period, and subscriptions auto-renew unless you cancel before renewal.

Do I have to buy leads from Instantly?

No. Outreach is for sending only. Instantly's lead database is a separate Credits subscription starting at $47 a month, or you can bundle it with Outreach from $94 a month. You can also upload leads sourced from other tools.

What does a 10-mailbox Instantly setup cost per month?

On our arithmetic, about $153 to $173 a month on Hypergrowth, depending on whether mailboxes come from Instantly's $5 done-for-you Google accounts or from Google Workspace at $7. That excludes lead data, CRM and verification. It is our estimate, not an Instantly quote.
