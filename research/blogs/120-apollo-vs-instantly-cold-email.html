  • Pick Apollo for an all-in-one seat. Data, calls and sequences share one login and one credit pool, with documented two-way HubSpot sync.

  • Many teams use both: Apollo or Clay for targeting, Instantly for sending, and verification in between.

“Apollo vs Instantly” sounds like a head-to-head, but the two tools overlap in the middle and differ at the edges. Apollo started as a contact database and grew into outreach and calling. Instantly started as a way to send cold email from many inboxes and grew a lead database. Which one is right depends on whether your bottleneck is finding the right people or landing messages in their inbox.

The Short Answer

If your main problem is volume and inbox placement across many domains, choose Instantly. If you want data, sequences and calling in one tool with close CRM sync and your volume is moderate, choose Apollo. If you can afford two tools, use Apollo or Clay for targeting and Instantly for sending.

Lead Data

Apollo’s homepage claims 240M+ contacts and 30M+ accounts. Instantly describes SuperSearch as 450M+ verified B2B leads with enrichment from several providers (directional). Do not read the larger number as better coverage: the vendors count different things, and neither figure is independently audited. The practical test is to export 200 leads from your own ICP from each and verify them. Apollo’s homepage does not describe a verification method, while Instantly lists checks for syntax, domain, mailbox existence, catch-all, disposable and role addresses, at a per-check credit cost (directional). Whichever you use, verify before sending; our email verification tools guide and bounce rate guide explain why.

Sending Infrastructure and Deliverability

This is where the tools differ most. Instantly lets you connect unlimited email accounts on its paid outreach plans, warms them in a private network, rotates senders automatically, and offers scheduled placement tests on Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo (directional). It also sells done-for-you domains and mailboxes (directional).

Apollo lists domain warming, inbox rotation and reputation monitoring on its homepage. Mailbox limits appear to depend on plan, with the entry tier allowing one mailbox per user (directional). Apollo’s guidance suggests starting around 50 emails a day per mailbox and adding mailboxes rather than raising one mailbox’s limit, though an older article gives a different default, so check the current setting (directional). Warmup is billed in credits (directional).

Warmup is not a guarantee. Both tools use shared warmup pools, and one independent review notes that a healthy warmup score can coexist with spam placement in live campaigns (directional). Authentication, list quality and sending volume per mailbox matter more. See our guides to cold email infrastructure and how many domains and mailboxes you need.

Sequencing, Testing and Replies

Both run multi-step email sequences. Apollo supports A/B testing of emails but not LinkedIn messages (directional). Instantly calls its version A/Z testing and lists it on higher tiers (directional). For replies, Instantly’s Unibox centralizes responses across all connected accounts with team assignment and AI labels (directional). We found no documented unified inbox in Apollo, which matters once you run many mailboxes.

Calls and LinkedIn

Apollo has a Parallel Dialer and, on its Professional plan, call recording. LinkedIn steps run as manual tasks rather than full automation (directional). Instantly offers calling and SMS only through a higher-tier CRM product and has no native LinkedIn automation (directional). If LinkedIn outreach is part of your motion, plan to add a dedicated tool; our cold email vs LinkedIn guide covers how the channels fit together.

CRM Sync

Apollo documents a two-way HubSpot sync with push conditions, field mapping and overwrite rules; the details are in our Apollo HubSpot integration guide. Instantly’s native HubSpot connection is one-way, with OutboundSync as the route to two-way sync (directional), which we cover in the Instantly HubSpot guide. Neither vendor documented a Close integration in the pages we could retrieve, so Close teams should read our Close cold email integration guide and test before committing.

Pricing Models

Apollo uses a shared credit pool per team. Credits cover emails, phone numbers, warmup, domains and mailboxes, and AI data, and they expire unused each cycle. Instantly sells flat monthly outreach plans with send and contact caps, plus a separate credit allowance for the lead database and enrichment. Because Apollo spends credits on infrastructure and Instantly does not, high mailbox counts tend to be where the models diverge. Model your own volume before comparing sticker prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for cold email at scale, Apollo or Instantly?

Instantly is built around sending: unlimited mailbox connections, rotation and placement tests (directional). Apollo’s mailbox caps and conservative per-mailbox limits suit lower volume.

Is Apollo’s database bigger than Instantly’s?

Each vendor claims its own number: Apollo says 240M+ contacts and 30M+ accounts, Instantly says 450M+ leads. Neither is independently audited, and the units differ, so do not treat them as a like-for-like comparison.

Do I need to verify emails before sending from either tool?

Yes. Instantly describes built-in verification that costs credits per check (directional), and Apollo’s own testimonial ties data accuracy to deliverability. Bounces hurt sender reputation either way.

Can I run calls and LinkedIn from Apollo or Instantly?

Apollo lists a Parallel Dialer and supports LinkedIn tasks (directional). Instantly offers calling and SMS only in a higher CRM tier and has no native LinkedIn automation (directional). For automated LinkedIn, teams add a dedicated tool.

Do Apollo and Instantly sync with HubSpot?

Apollo describes a two-way HubSpot sync with field mapping. Instantly’s native connection is one-way, and OutboundSync is the route to two-way (directional). Neither vendor documented a Close integration in the pages we could retrieve.

Can I use Apollo and Instantly together?

Yes. A common setup uses Apollo for targeting and enrichment and Instantly for sending, with emails verified in between and outcomes synced to the CRM. It adds a second tool to maintain, so only do it if volume justifies it.
