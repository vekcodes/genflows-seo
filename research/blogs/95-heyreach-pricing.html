The Short Answer

If you run fewer than about 13 LinkedIn accounts, Growth is the cheaper plan: $79 per sender monthly or $63 on yearly billing. Past that point Agency's flat $999 (or $799 yearly) is cheaper per sender and adds whitelabel and onboarding. Unlimited only makes sense for large agencies running dozens of accounts. Whatever you pick, budget separately for LinkedIn Premium or Sales Navigator on each account and for the data that feeds the campaigns.

What a sender is

HeyReach defines a sender (or seat) as a LinkedIn account you add to your organization. It says it does not charge for teammates, VAs, clients or users; only for connected LinkedIn accounts. That is the opposite of most sales tools, and it is why agencies with many client accounts feel the per-sender model quickly.

What every plan includes

  • Unlimited campaigns and all LinkedIn actions.
  • A unified inbox across senders.
  • API and webhooks, plus integrations with Clay, Make, Zapier and HubSpot. Our Clay + HeyReach walkthrough shows the handoff in detail.
  • Multichannel hooks with Instantly and Smartlead, and an MCP server.

Agency and Unlimited add whitelabel, done-for-you onboarding and migration, a dedicated Slack channel and priority support. Multi-brand whitelabels are a $500 add-on.

Beware stale prices. Several third-party review pages we found quote different numbers, such as Growth at $59 and Agency at $749 on annual billing. They disagree with HeyReach's own page, so treat them as (directional) and out of date. The vendor page is the number you will be charged.

Trial, Billing and Refunds

  • Growth trial: 14 days, no credit card, with up to 3 LinkedIn accounts.
  • Agency and Unlimited trial: a 30-day trial is available on request. The pricing page wording was not fully consistent about which plans it covers, so ask before you rely on it (directional).
  • Refunds: subscriptions are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started. Requests such as duplicate billing, charges after cancellation or a wrong seat count are reviewed case by case.
  • Cancelling: stops renewal but gives no immediate refund. Active campaigns pause and accounts disconnect.
  • Changing plans: upgrades mid-cycle are charged pro rata for the remaining days. Downgrades and seat reductions take effect at the next renewal.
  • Pausing: a help article snippet suggests you can pause for up to two months without billing (directional, we did not read the full article).

What Each Seat Can Actually Do: LinkedIn Limits

A seat's price only makes sense against how much it can send. HeyReach publishes these limits:

Limit HeyReach setting
Connection requests per day Up to 40; recommended 25 for warmed accounts
Connection requests per week 200 per sender (another help article says around 100, so plan on the lower figure (directional))
Total actions per day 200 per account, all LinkedIn activity counts
Personalized connection notes 10 a month on free LinkedIn accounts, unlimited on Premium, Sales Navigator and Recruiter
Proxy Dedicated static residential proxy per account

When an account hits the weekly limit, HeyReach's Cooldown Mode stops requests until Monday, and it cannot be removed manually. Limits are enforced with no manual override. The practical takeaway is that one seat gives you roughly 100 to 200 new connection requests a week, which is what you are really buying at $63 to $79. Unlimited's fair-use cap of 300 senders implies under $10 per sender at full use, but that is arithmetic on a cap, not a plan promise.

The hidden line item. Personalized connection notes are unlimited only on paid LinkedIn accounts, so most serious senders also carry a Premium or Sales Navigator subscription. Our Sales Navigator pricing guide covers that cost, and it is per account, on top of HeyReach.

How the Pricing Model Compares

We compared structure only, not features:

  • Expandi: a per-seat model like Growth. The Business plan is $99 a month or $79 a month annually, with custom Agency pricing for 10 or more seats. That is above HeyReach Growth at every billing period.
  • Waalaxy: priced per user in euros (Pro €19, Advanced €49, Business €69 a month) with monthly invite caps of 300 on Pro and 800 on higher tiers. It bills per user rather than per LinkedIn account, so the comparison depends on how many accounts you run.
  • Lemlist and Dripify: we could not confirm current pricing, so we are not quoting it.

The structural difference that matters is who counts as a seat. HeyReach's Agency and Unlimited tiers reward many accounts and many clients; per-user tools reward small teams with one account each. Is HeyReach Worth It?

For a single founder or a small team with two or three senders, Growth on yearly billing is a modest spend, around $189 a month for three. The bigger question is whether LinkedIn is the channel to put volume into. A seat delivers a bounded number of connection requests, so pipeline scales with senders and reply quality, not with the plan tier. Frequently Asked Questions

How is HeyReach priced?

Per LinkedIn sender account on the Growth plan, at $79 a month, $71 on quarterly billing or $63 on yearly billing. HeyReach does not charge for users, VAs or clients. Agency and Unlimited are flat plans that cover a set number of senders.

How many senders does the Agency plan include?

Agency includes 25 senders at $999 a month ($799 a month on yearly billing) and can scale to 50. Unlimited has a fair-use cap of 300 senders at $2,999 a month.

When is the Agency plan cheaper than Growth?

From about 13 senders. That is our arithmetic: $999 divided by $79 is roughly 12.6 on monthly billing, and $799 divided by $63 is roughly 12.7 on yearly billing. Agency also adds whitelabel, onboarding help and a dedicated Slack channel.

Does HeyReach have a free trial?

Growth has a 14-day trial with no credit card and up to 3 LinkedIn accounts. A 30-day trial is available on request for Agency and Unlimited, though the pricing page wording was not fully consistent about which plans it covers, so confirm with HeyReach first.

Can I get a refund on HeyReach?

Generally not once a billing period has started. Cases such as duplicate billing, charges after cancellation or a wrong seat count are reviewed individually. Cancelling stops renewal but does not refund the current period.

What LinkedIn limits does HeyReach enforce?

Up to 40 connection requests a day, with 25 recommended for warmed accounts, and a weekly cap of 200 per sender (one help article says around 100). Each account also has a 200-action daily cap covering all activity, and each gets a dedicated static residential proxy. Limits cannot be overridden manually.
