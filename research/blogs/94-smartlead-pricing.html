TL;DR

  • Four plans: Base $39/mo, Pro $94/mo, Unlimited Smart $174/mo and Unlimited Prime $379/mo on monthly billing. Annual billing takes roughly 17% off.
  • The plan is priced by volume, not by mailbox. Smartlead's pricing page advertises unlimited email accounts at no extra cost. Sends per month (6,000 to 500,000) and contact storage are what separate the tiers.

The Short Answer

Most small outbound teams land on Smartlead Pro at $94 a month ($78.30 on annual billing), which covers 90,000 sends and 30,000 contacts. Once you count mailboxes and domains, a 10-mailbox program costs roughly $144 to $169 a month all-in and a 30-mailbox program roughly $245 to $320, before verification and any deliverability add-ons. Base is for testing, Unlimited Smart is for teams that want unlimited contact storage and included verification, and Prime is for agencies that need private infrastructure and client workspaces.

What separates the tiers

  • Base to Pro is a jump from 6,000 to 90,000 monthly sends. Base is a testing tier: 6,000 sends is roughly 200 a day, which is only a few mailboxes at conservative volumes.
  • Pro to Smart removes the contact-storage cap and bundles 50,000 verified emails a month, which matters if you build large lists.
  • Smart to Prime adds 3 SmartServers with OAuth, private infrastructure, a dedicated manager, a private Slack channel and 3 or more client workspaces.
  • Mailboxes are not a tier lever. Smartlead's pricing page says unlimited email accounts at no extra cost. You still pay whoever hosts the mailboxes.

Check the feature matrix before you buy. Sources disagreed on whether warmup is included on Base, and on which tiers include API and webhook access. We are not stating either. Confirm both on the live pricing page if they decide your plan.

Trial and Refund Rules

  • Free trial: 14 days, no credit card, capped at 2,500 sends and 1,250 contact storage. Warmup emails do not count toward sends, and white label is excluded.
  • New subscriptions: refundable within 7 days of payment.
  • Renewals: refundable within 3 days, most recent renewal only.
  • Upgrades: not refundable, though you can downgrade.
  • Non-refundable add-ons: SmartSenders, SmartInfra, SmartDelivery, SmartProspects, email verification and SmartDialer.

The practical lesson: do not buy a large SmartSenders batch on day one of a trial. Domains and mailboxes cannot be refunded, so validate the campaign on a small set first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Smartlead cost in 2026?

Smartlead plans run from $39 a month (Base) to $379 a month (Unlimited Prime) on monthly billing, with roughly 17% off on annual billing. Pro, the plan most small teams use, is $94 a month. Mailboxes, domains and add-ons are extra.

Does Smartlead limit the number of mailboxes?

Smartlead's pricing page advertises unlimited email accounts at no extra cost. Plans are limited by monthly sends and contact storage instead. You still pay for the mailboxes themselves, from about $4.50 a month each through SmartSenders or from Google or Microsoft directly.

Is there a Smartlead free trial or refund?

There is a 14-day free trial with no credit card, capped at 2,500 sends. Separately, new paid subscriptions have a 7-day refund window. Add-ons such as SmartSenders, SmartDelivery and email verification are non-refundable.

What does a 10-mailbox Smartlead setup cost per month?

On our arithmetic, about $144 to $169 a month on the Pro plan, depending on whether mailboxes come from SmartSenders at $4.50 or from Google Workspace at $7. That excludes email verification and other add-ons. It is our estimate, not a Smartlead quote.

What extras does Smartlead charge for?

SmartServers cost $39 each a month, SmartDelivery inbox-placement testing costs $49 to $599 a month, email verification starts at $32 a month, extra client workspaces are $29 each, and SmartSenders sells mailboxes and domains separately. Verification is included on the Smart and Prime plans.

Which Smartlead plan should I choose?

Base suits testing at about 6,000 sends a month. Pro suits most single-company programs at up to 90,000 sends. Unlimited Smart suits teams building large lists that want bundled verification. Prime suits agencies that need client workspaces and private infrastructure.
