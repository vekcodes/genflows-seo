TL;DR

  • Instantly is the email-volume tool; lemlist is the multichannel tool. That is the whole decision for most teams, and the rest of this post tests it.
  • Instantly Outreach starts at $47/month ($37.60/month billed yearly) with unlimited mailboxes and warmup on every plan; Hypergrowth is $97 ($77.60 yearly) for 125,000 emails/month.
  • lemlist Email is $55/month on yearly billing (50,000 emails/month, email only); Multichannel is $87 on yearly billing, priced per user, and adds LinkedIn, SMS and calls.
  • LinkedIn and calls are the clearest divider. lemlist lists LinkedIn steps, SMS, in-app calling and a power dialer on its pricing page. We did not find LinkedIn, SMS or call steps on Instantly’s pricing page, so confirm before assuming either way.
  • Credits are a second bill on both. Instantly and lemlist each sell lead data on credits. Neither’s sticker price includes the leads you will actually burn.
  • CRM fit differs. lemlist lists two-way HubSpot and Salesforce sync plus Pipedrive and Close. Instantly documents HubSpot and Pipedrive connections; Salesforce goes through a third party and Close through Zapier.

Instantly and lemlist are two of the most common names in cold outreach shortlists, and they are often compared as if they were the same product at different prices. They are not. One is optimised to push high volumes of email through many mailboxes cheaply. The other is optimised to sequence a prospect across email, LinkedIn and phone. Which one is “better” depends entirely on which problem you have.

We run cold email and LinkedIn outbound for B2B clients, so this comparison is built around what changes your results and your bill. We already publish detailed pricing guides for Instantly and lemlist, and a head-to-head of Smartlead vs Instantly; for the wider field see our best cold email software roundup.

The Short Answer

Choose Instantly if your programme is email-first and you need volume: many mailboxes, many domains, high monthly send counts and the lowest cost per email. Unlimited mailboxes and warmup on every Outreach plan is what makes it the default for agencies and high-volume senders.

Choose lemlist if the sequence itself is multichannel — an email, a LinkedIn visit and invite, a voice note, then a call — and you want that in one tool with two-way CRM sync. You pay per user for that capability, and you accept lower raw email volume per dollar.

Use both only if you have a reason: some teams send volume email from Instantly and run a small, high-touch account list through lemlist. For most, running two sequencers just splits your reply data.

Channels: Email-Only vs Multichannel

lemlist (verified on its pricing page): Multichannel and Enterprise include LinkedIn automation (profile visits, invites, messages, voice notes, InMail), SMS, in-app calling with a power dialer and VoIP integrations (Aircall, Ringover). The Email plan is email only. LinkedIn limits suggested in lemlist help content are about 20 invites, 30 profile visits and 30 messages a day (directional — we saw this in summary); keep to conservative limits whichever tool you use. Our LinkedIn limits and account safety guide explains why.

Instantly: it is positioned as email-first, and we found no LinkedIn, SMS or call steps on its homepage or pricing page. Treat that as “not found” rather than proof they do not exist, and verify in a trial if you need them.

If LinkedIn is a major channel for you, the stronger pattern is usually a dedicated LinkedIn tool alongside your email sender — see our LinkedIn automation tools comparison and cold email vs LinkedIn outreach.

Deliverability and Infrastructure

  • Instantly: unlimited warmup and unlimited mailboxes on every Outreach plan, plus bounce detection and reputation protection. It also sells done-for-you infrastructure; help content lists domains at about $15/year and pre-warmed Google accounts at about $10/month with 24–72 hour setup (directional — re-check live prices).
  • lemlist: lemwarm and deliverability protections are included on all plans. Help guidance suggests not launching a campaign below a warmup score of about 90 and keeping warmup plus campaign sends to roughly 50 emails a day per mailbox (directional). We found no done-for-you inbox product.

The honest point on deliverability: neither tool saves a bad list or a bad domain setup. Infrastructure, authentication and volume discipline matter more than the sequencer. Start with our SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup guide and how many domains and mailboxes you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Instantly or lemlist better for cold email?

Instantly is usually better for high-volume, email-only programmes because every Outreach plan includes unlimited mailboxes and warmup. lemlist is usually better when sequences mix email with LinkedIn and calls, and when you want two-way CRM sync.

Which is cheaper, Instantly or lemlist?

At entry level Instantly Growth is $47/month ($37.60 billed yearly) against lemlist Email at $55/month on yearly billing, but Instantly caps Growth at 5,000 emails while lemlist Email allows 50,000. Compare at your real send volume, and add lead credits to both.

Can I do LinkedIn outreach with Instantly or lemlist?

lemlist Multichannel includes LinkedIn steps, SMS and calling. We did not find LinkedIn steps on Instantly’s pricing page, so confirm in a trial or pair Instantly with a dedicated LinkedIn tool.

Do both include email warmup?

Yes. Instantly includes unlimited warmup on all Outreach plans and lemlist includes lemwarm on all plans. Warmup does not fix poor authentication, bad lists or aggressive volume.

Do Instantly and lemlist include lead data?

Both offer lead databases (Instantly claims 450M+ and lemlist 650M+) but bill them separately on credits. Budget for credits in addition to your sending plan.

Which integrates better with HubSpot or Salesforce?

lemlist lists two-way sync with both HubSpot and Salesforce. Instantly documents HubSpot and Pipedrive connections and reaches Salesforce through a third party. Always test what actually logs to the CRM before relying on it for reporting.
