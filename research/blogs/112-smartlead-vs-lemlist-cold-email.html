The Short Answer

Choose Smartlead if you send email at volume, want unlimited mailboxes without per-seat pricing, or run outbound for several clients and need white-label workspaces and a shared inbox.

Choose lemlist if you want LinkedIn steps, calls and optional WhatsApp in the same sequence builder, and a built-in lead database so you can prospect and send from one login.

If LinkedIn is a large part of your motion, a dedicated tool such as HeyReach alongside either email sender is the more common setup at scale (directional). See our LinkedIn automation tools comparison.

Pricing Compared

Smartlead

Smartlead lists four plans: Base ($39 monthly or $32 annual; 6,000 emails a month, 2,000 active leads), Pro ($94 or $78; 90,000 emails, 30,000 leads), Unlimited Smart ($174 or $144; 150,000 emails) and Unlimited Prime ($379 or $315; 500,000 emails). The page says annual billing saves 17%. Unlimited email accounts are included on every plan, so the real limits are monthly send volume and active leads. Mailboxes bought through its SmartSenders marketplace cost extra, roughly $3.99 to $9 per mailbox per month plus domain fees. See our Smartlead pricing breakdown.

lemlist

lemlist lists Email ($69 monthly or $55 annual), Multichannel ($109 or $87) and a custom Enterprise plan. The Email plan includes unlimited senders and 50,000 emails a month; Multichannel adds LinkedIn automation and a call dialer, with WhatsApp as an add-on. Lead enrichment credits are a separate add-on ($20 a month for 2,000 credits as listed). The page does not make clear whether the headline prices are per user, and one search snippet quoted a higher Email price, so check the live page (directional). See our lemlist pricing breakdown.

Mailboxes, Volume and Infrastructure

Smartlead’s pitch is simple: add unlimited mailboxes, store unlimited leads and pay for what you send. That suits teams that spread volume across many mailboxes and domains. lemlist’s Email plan also lists unlimited senders, while Multichannel lists five senders per user, so the cap you hit depends on the plan.

More mailboxes do not mean more safe volume by themselves. Keep per-mailbox sending low and spread across domains. Our guides on how many domains and mailboxes you need and mailbox rotation cover the numbers, and cold email infrastructure covers the setup.

Deliverability Features

Smartlead advertises built-in warmup, sender and IP rotation, and a separate SmartDelivery product with inbox-placement tests across Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo, blacklist monitoring across 400+ lists and spam-filter scoring. SmartDelivery is a paid add-on, listed from $49 a month. Rotation and dedicated-IP claims are marketing wording on Smartlead’s site, so treat results as (directional).

lemlist includes lemwarm, its warmup product, and on the pages we read we found no placement-testing product. Some bundle terms for lemwarm depend on plan and sending volume (directional).

Whichever you pick, monitor placement independently of the sender. See cold email deliverability monitoring and our SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup guide.

Multichannel: LinkedIn, Calls and WhatsApp

This is lemlist’s clearest advantage. Multichannel and Enterprise include LinkedIn automation, Multichannel has a built-in dialer, and WhatsApp is an add-on. Smartlead is email-first: on the pages we reviewed we found no native LinkedIn automation or WhatsApp, only a SmartDialer credit add-on. That is absence of evidence, so confirm with Smartlead if LinkedIn steps matter.

Our view: LinkedIn automation inside an email tool is convenient, but account safety limits still apply whichever tool sends. Read our LinkedIn outreach limits guide and compare the two channels before you pick.

Lead Data

lemlist advertises a 650M+ lead database on all plans with enrichment credits sold as an add-on. We found no equivalent database in Smartlead; it offers verified-email quotas per plan and an email verification add-on, and expects you to source data elsewhere, for example from Clay. If you build lists separately, see how to build a cold email list and our email verification tools guide.

Agencies and Teams

Smartlead is built with agencies in mind: client workspaces from the Pro tier up, white-label as a $29 per workspace add-on, and a shared inbox across campaigns and clients. We found no white-label offering from lemlist on its pages. If you run outbound for others, that gap matters.

CRM Integration

Smartlead has a native HubSpot integration that syncs leads, creates deals and logs activity, but does not move deals between stages. lemlist lists a 2-way HubSpot sync. For Close, we found no official Smartlead connector (third-party tools and Zapier exist), and lemlist lists Close on its integrations page without making clear whether it is native (directional). Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper, Smartlead or lemlist?

On the prices we read, Smartlead starts lower: $39 a month ($32 annual) against lemlist Email at $69 ($55 annual). Smartlead’s higher tiers rise to $379, and add-ons such as SmartDelivery or lemlist enrichment credits change the total, so compare full stacks at your sending volume.

Do both offer unlimited mailboxes?

Smartlead includes unlimited email accounts on every plan. lemlist’s Email plan lists unlimited senders, but Multichannel lists five senders per user.

Which has a built-in lead database?

lemlist advertises 650M+ leads with enrichment credits as an add-on. We found no equivalent database in Smartlead, which offers verified-email quotas and integrations such as Clay.

Which handles LinkedIn, calls and WhatsApp?

lemlist’s Multichannel plan includes LinkedIn automation and a built-in dialer, with WhatsApp as an add-on. Smartlead is email-first; we found no native LinkedIn automation on the pages we reviewed.

Which is better for agencies?

Smartlead. It offers client workspaces, white-label as a $29 per workspace add-on and a shared inbox. We found no comparable white-label feature at lemlist.

Do they have free trials or refunds?

Both offer trials: Smartlead with no card required and an unstated length, lemlist 14 days with promotions that extend it. We could not find refund terms for either, so ask the vendor before buying.
