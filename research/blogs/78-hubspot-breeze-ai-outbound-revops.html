The Short Answer

HubSpot Breeze is an umbrella brand for three distinct things: a free AI assistant embedded across the CRM, a set of paid AI agents that complete specific jobs (the outbound-relevant one is the Prospecting Agent), and a data-enrichment layer called Breeze Intelligence. For a cold outbound and RevOps agency's purposes, the useful summary is: Breeze Assistant is a nice-to-have that costs nothing, the Prospecting Agent is a real but narrow tool for outreach to contacts already in your CRM, and none of it replaces dedicated cold-email or LinkedIn-sending infrastructure. The RevOps side — data hygiene, enrichment, deal-stage recommendations — is where Breeze currently pulls more weight.

What HubSpot Breeze Actually Is (Three Products, One Brand)

"Breeze" gets used loosely in vendor content to mean almost any AI feature in HubSpot. HubSpot's own knowledge base splits it into three pillars:

  • Breeze Assistant (renamed from "Breeze Copilot" at INBOUND 2025) — a free, in-app AI helper included on every HubSpot subscription, including Free. It drafts content, summarizes meetings and calls, answers questions about your CRM data, and pulls how-to guidance from HubSpot Academy. It integrates with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and mobile.
  • Breeze Agents (now organized under "Agent Hub") — paid, task-completing AI that runs multi-step work autonomously or semi-autonomously. The named agents live today: Prospecting Agent, Customer Agent, Knowledge Base Agent, Content Agent, Data Agent, and Deal Progression, plus an AI-search-visibility (AEO) agent.
  • Breeze Intelligence — the enrichment and intent-data layer: fills in firmographic/contact fields, surfaces buying-intent signals, and shortens forms. It draws from the same shared HubSpot Credits pool as the agents.
The trap: treating Breeze Assistant and Breeze Agents as the same thing. Assistant is free and conversational — you ask it something and it answers. Agents are paid, tier-gated, and complete a defined job (research a lead, resolve a ticket) with an outcome-based price tag. A lot of "is Breeze free?" confusion comes from conflating the two.

The Outbound Piece: What the Prospecting Agent Actually Does

The Prospecting Agent is the Breeze feature most relevant to a cold-outbound motion. It researches accounts and contacts using your CRM data plus connected enrichment providers, surfaces likely buying-committee contacts, monitors signals like funding events and leadership changes, and drafts personalized outreach emails in a rep's voice. It can run in a semi-autonomous mode (a rep reviews and approves before sending) or a fully autonomous mode (it sends without review).

Two things matter more than the feature list:

  • It sends through HubSpot's own channel only. Outreach goes out via your connected inbox or HubSpot sequences — there's no native path for it to send a LinkedIn message. A HubSpot sequence can include a "send LinkedIn connection request" step, but that's a task reminder for a human, not something the agent executes for you.
  • It requires Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise. It isn't available on Starter or Free, though HubSpot has offered a complimentary 28-day trial period.

How Much It Costs — HubSpot Moved to Outcome-Based Pricing in April 2026

As of April 14, 2026, HubSpot changed how it charges for its two flagship agents: you pay when the agent completes the job, not a flat monthly fee on top of your seat.

What Breeze Does Not Do — the Part Vendor Pages Skip

This is the section most relevant if you're running or buying cold outbound:

AssumptionReality
"Breeze can replace Smartlead/Instantly for cold sending"HubSpot's own sequence send limits cap Professional seats at 500 emails/day and Enterprise seats at 1,000/day, throttled to roughly 3 emails/minute during bulk enrollment. There's no dedicated domain-warmup network built in.
"Breeze automates LinkedIn outreach"No agent sends LinkedIn messages. A sequence can prompt a rep with a manual LinkedIn task; nothing executes it automatically.
"Cold outreach through HubSpot is against the rules"HubSpot's Acceptable Use Policy doesn't name-ban cold email outright, but it prohibits sending spam and harvesting email addresses without consent, and requires compliance with CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and CASL — in practice the binding constraint for high-volume cold sending is the technical send caps above, not an explicit policy line.
"There's a Breeze forecasting agent"We could not find one by that name in HubSpot's own materials. Forecasting-adjacent capability currently lives inside Deal Progression recommendations and standard Sales Hub reporting, not a discrete named agent.

None of this makes Breeze bad at outbound — it means Breeze's outreach layer is scoped to accounts and contacts you're already tracking in HubSpot, sent at HubSpot-sanctioned volume. If your model depends on cold-sending thousands of net-new contacts a month across rotating domains, that's a job for a dedicated stack; see our comparison of HubSpot Sequences vs. dedicated cold-email tools for where that line sits in more detail.

The RevOps Side: Data Agent, Breeze Intelligence, Deal Progression

Breeze pulls more clean weight on the RevOps side, where the job is less "generate new outreach" and more "keep the data and pipeline honest":

  • Data Agent — works on CRM data quality: enrichment and maintenance tasks like deduping and filling incomplete fields, consuming credits from the same pool as the outreach agents.
  • Breeze Intelligence — fills in firmographic and contact data, surfaces buyer-intent signals, and can shorten forms by auto-filling fields it already knows. This is the piece most directly comparable to a waterfall-enrichment setup like Clay's HubSpot sync, though it operates natively inside HubSpot rather than as an external tool feeding the CRM.
  • Deal Progression — surfaces AI recommendations for advancing deals through your pipeline, which only works well if your deal stages are actually structured for how your team sells in the first place. Garbage-in, garbage-out applies here more than anywhere else in Breeze.
Note: HubSpot states its own activation numbers for these agents (for example, tens of thousands of customers have activated the Prospecting and Customer Agents, with double-digit resolution rates and quarter-over-quarter growth cited in its own company announcements) — these are HubSpot's self-reported figures, not independently audited, and we have not found any published data isolating outbound pipeline or revenue lift specifically attributable to the Prospecting Agent. Treat vendor-published adoption stats as directional, not proof of ROI for your specific pipeline.

Common Setup Mistakes We See

  • Turning on the Prospecting Agent with sparse CRM data. It drafts from what it has — thin contact/company records and no clear ICP or value-prop notes produce generic emails, agent or not.
  • Running cold volume through HubSpot Sequences because "it's already there." Sequences are tuned for warm/known contacts; pushing high-volume cold sends through them risks the sender's domain reputation and runs into the daily caps above faster than expected.
  • Sending AI drafts unedited. Whether from Breeze or any other AI drafting tool, unedited outputs sent verbatim tend to underperform simpler, human-tightened copy — treat the agent's draft as a first pass, not a final one.
  • Assuming a rep can prospect entirely inside HubSpot. Real outbound workflows span LinkedIn and other channels Breeze doesn't reach — plan the handoff between HubSpot and your outbound tool stack deliberately instead of assuming Breeze covers it end to end.

Where to Start This Week

  1. Confirm your Hub tier — Prospecting Agent and Customer Agent both require Professional or Enterprise, and Breeze Assistant is already available to you for free regardless.
  2. Fix deal stages and CRM data hygiene before turning on Data Agent or Deal Progression — both amplify whatever structure and data quality already exists rather than fixing it for you.
  3. If you're running cold outbound at volume, keep it on dedicated infrastructure and use HubSpot/Breeze for what it's built for: enrichment, CRM hygiene, and outreach to contacts already in your funnel.
  4. Check your account's actual included credit allotment and current per-outcome pricing in-portal before rolling an agent out to a full team — HubSpot has changed both the naming and the pricing model within the last year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does HubSpot Breeze replace a cold email tool like Smartlead or Instantly?

No. The Prospecting Agent drafts and can send outreach, but only through HubSpot's own connected-inbox and sequence infrastructure, which caps at 500–1,000 emails per day per seat and has no dedicated domain-warmup network. It's built for CRM-integrated outreach to contacts you're already tracking, not high-volume cold-sending infrastructure.

Can HubSpot Breeze automate LinkedIn outreach?

No. Breeze's agents send through HubSpot's own channels, primarily email. A HubSpot sequence can include a LinkedIn step, but it's a manual task reminder for a rep to complete — nothing in Breeze sends a LinkedIn message automatically.

What HubSpot tier do I need for Breeze AI agents?

Breeze Assistant is included free on every tier, including Free. The task-completing agents — Prospecting Agent, Customer Agent, and others under Agent Hub — require Professional or Enterprise on the relevant Hub, plus HubSpot Credits to pay for outcomes.

How does HubSpot charge for Breeze AI agents?

As of April 14, 2026, HubSpot uses outcome-based pricing paid in HubSpot Credits: $1.00 per lead the Prospecting Agent recommends for outreach (100 credits), and $0.50 per conversation the Customer Agent resolves (50 credits). Credits reset monthly, don't roll over, and overage bills at $0.01 per credit.

Is Breeze Copilot the same as Breeze Agents?

No, and this is the most common mix-up. Breeze Assistant (renamed from Breeze Copilot in 2025) is a free, in-app helper you ask questions and get drafts from on demand. Breeze Agents are paid, tier-gated systems that autonomously complete multi-step jobs, like researching a prospect and sending outreach or resolving a support ticket.

Does Breeze Intelligence handle CRM data enrichment for RevOps?

Yes — it enriches contact and company records and surfaces buyer-intent signals, drawing from the same shared HubSpot Credits pool as the AI agents. Exact included-credit allotments vary by subscription tier, so check your account's live catalog page rather than assuming a fixed number.
