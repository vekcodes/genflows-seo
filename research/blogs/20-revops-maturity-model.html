The Self-Scoring Rubric (Four Dimensions)

Score each dimension 1–5 honestly — "we have a doc somewhere" doesn't count as documented. Your effective maturity is your lowest score. A team with tooling at 4 and data at 1 operates at 1 with expensive software.

How to Advance One Stage (Not Three)

  • 1 → 2: Get everything into one CRM. Every contact, every deal, every touchpoint. Don't optimize anything yet — you can't improve what isn't captured. The startup minimum viable stack is the shopping list.
  • 2 → 3: Write the definitions down. Lead, MQL, SQL, opportunity, every stage's entry/exit criteria — one page, signed by sales and marketing. Then make the CRM enforce it with required fields. This is the least glamorous and highest-ROI move in all of RevOps.
  • 3 → 4: Instrument the funnel. Timestamps on handoffs, source on every record, and a dashboard reviewed weekly. Build it in this order: the 12-metric RevOps dashboard.
  • 4 → 5: Earn the right to predict. Once you have four quarters of clean funnel data, forecast models and revenue experiments stop being theater. Layer AI here — not before — and consolidate tooling as you go (the 2026 stack guide covers the 3–4-platform consolidation play).

The stage-skipping failure mode is always the same: a tool purchased two stages early. Forecasting AI at stage 2 predicts confidently from garbage. Attribution software at stage 1 attributes revenue to noise. Match the purchase to the stage and half the stack budget frees itself up.

Where Outbound Fits

Maturity isn't only an inbound story. If pipeline comes from outbound, the same rubric applies upstream of the CRM: list quality is a data score, sequencing is a process score, and deliverability monitoring is instrumentation. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a RevOps maturity model?

A framework that maps how developed your revenue operations are — from reactive and siloed to predictive and integrated — so you can see which capability to build next. Ours uses five stages (Chaotic, Organized, Standardized, Measured, Predictive) scored across process, data, tooling, and alignment.

How do I assess our RevOps maturity?

Score each of the four dimensions 1–5 against the rubric above, honestly. Your effective maturity is your lowest dimension score, not the average — a single weak dimension caps what the others can deliver.

What stage are most companies at?

The majority of B2B organizations sit at stage 2–3: CRM adopted and tools connected, but definitions inconsistent and reporting backward-looking. Most teams also self-assess one stage higher than they score.

How long does it take to move up a stage?

Stage 1→2 is a quarter of focused CRM migration. Stage 2→3 is 4–6 weeks of definition work plus enforcement. Stage 3→4 takes a quarter to instrument and a quarter of dashboard reviews to bed in. Stage 4→5 requires several quarters of clean data before models are trustworthy.

Should we buy AI forecasting tools?

Only at stage 4 or later. Forecasting models trained on unstandardized stages and incomplete records produce confident nonsense. Fix definitions and instrumentation first — the model is the last mile, not the first.
