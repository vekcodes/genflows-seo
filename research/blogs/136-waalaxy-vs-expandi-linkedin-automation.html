TL;DR

  • Price: Waalaxy lists €19, €49 and €69 per user; Expandi is $99 per month, or $79 billed annually.
  • Architecture: Waalaxy runs as a browser extension; Expandi is cloud-based with a dedicated country IP.
  • Email: Waalaxy adds cold email sequences on its Business plan; Expandi's email support is unconfirmed.
  • Agencies: Expandi has a white-label Agency plan (10+ seats); Waalaxy has no agency plan.
  • Pick: Waalaxy for budget and built-in email; Expandi for cloud execution and agency work.

Waalaxy and Expandi are two of the best-known LinkedIn outreach tools, but they are built differently. One is a low-priced extension with an email add-on, the other a pricier cloud tool aimed at agencies. This guide compares them on the points that decide a purchase.

Pricing, trial and plan details were read on each vendor's pricing page in October 2026. Limits and safety claims come from vendor blogs and marketing, which conflict in places, so they are labelled (directional). Confirm on the live pages before you buy.

The Short Answer

Choose Waalaxy if you are a solo user or small team that wants the lowest entry price and optional cold email in the same tool. Choose Expandi if you want actions to run in the cloud on a dedicated IP, need conditional sequences, or run LinkedIn for several clients and want white-label reporting. Neither vendor offers an independent safety guarantee, so treat both as tools that reduce risk rather than remove it.

Pricing: Per-User Euros vs Per-Seat Dollars

Waalaxy is cheaper on paper at every tier. Pro is €19, Advanced €49 and Business €69, each per user per month, with monthly invite allowances of 300, 800 and 800. Expandi has a single self-serve plan at $99 a month, or $79 billed annually (paid upfront), plus a custom Agency plan.

The comparison is closer than the headline numbers suggest. Waalaxy's lower plans exclude email sequences, and its LinkedIn Inbox is a €20 add-on. Expandi bundles cloud execution, sequences and a shared inbox into one price. For a full breakdown, see our Waalaxy pricing and Expandi pricing guides.

Extension vs Cloud: What It Changes

Waalaxy describes its product as an extension, so your browser has to be running for campaigns to work. Expandi says there is no Chrome extension and that actions run server-side, with a consistent country-specific IP per account and an automatic profile warm-up.

Cloud is not the same as safe. Expandi's own blog says none of its protective layers is a guarantee. We found no independent ban-rate data for either tool, so account risk depends more on your volumes and targeting than on the vendor. Sequences, Email and Inbox

Expandi is the stronger LinkedIn-only builder: an If/Action sequence builder, ten campaign types, unlimited campaigns and step-by-step stats. Waalaxy offers pre-built sequences and, on Business, adds cold email and LinkedIn-plus-email multichannel with 500 email-finder credits a month. If email is part of your plan, Waalaxy bundles it; with Expandi you would typically run email in a dedicated sender. See cold email vs LinkedIn outreach and our cold email software comparison.

Integrations and CRM Sync

Waalaxy lists CRM sync from Pro and API plus Make, Zapier and n8n modules from Advanced. Expandi points to webhooks for CRM connections and we did not find Zapier or named native CRMs on its pages. Whichever you choose, the harder part is routing leads, replies and statuses into your CRM cleanly. Our Waalaxy and Expandi HubSpot integration guide and the guide on syncing cold email and LinkedIn data into HubSpot cover this.

Agencies and Teams

Expandi's Agency plan starts at 10 seats and adds roles and permissions, client reports, template sharing, a customer success manager and white-label use under your own brand. Waalaxy has no agency plan: Enterprise, for five or more seats, adds team workspaces, volume discounts and onboarding, but no white label is stated. If you run many client accounts, also look at HeyReach, which is built around multi-account sending.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper, Waalaxy or Expandi?

Waalaxy starts lower at €19 per user per month, against $79 to $99 for Expandi. Waalaxy's page does not say which billing cycle the headline prices reflect, so check the toggle. Waalaxy's email sequences require the €69 Business plan.

Is Waalaxy cloud-based or a browser extension?

Waalaxy describes its product as an extension. We found no statement about a cloud component. Expandi says it is cloud-based with no extension to install.

Does either tool support email outreach?

Waalaxy's Business plan includes cold email sequences and LinkedIn-plus-email multichannel. Expandi markets omnichannel campaigns, but we could not confirm native email sending.

Can agencies manage multiple clients?

Expandi's Agency plan (10 or more seats, custom price) includes roles, client reports and white label. Waalaxy has no agency plan; Enterprise starts at five seats without a stated white-label option.

What are the safe daily limits?

Vendor figures conflict across pages and dates, so treat them as guidance. Waalaxy's blog cites 80 to 100 invites a day and a 200-per-week limit; Expandi's guidance is 15 to 20 invites per active day and about 100 per week (directional). Start low and ramp.

Is there a free trial or refund?

Waalaxy offers 14 days and Expandi 7 days. Neither pricing page states a refund policy, so assume payments are not refundable and cancel before renewal.
