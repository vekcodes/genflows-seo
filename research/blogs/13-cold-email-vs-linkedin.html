"Should we do cold email or LinkedIn?" is one of the most common questions we get — and it's slightly the wrong question. Both are just delivery channels for the same thing: a relevant message to the right person at the right time. The channel that wins is the one that gets your message in front of your buyer with the least friction. Cold Email vs LinkedIn: The Honest Benchmarks

Reply rates are the headline, and here LinkedIn genuinely wins. Across 2026 industry data, personalized LinkedIn outreach commonly returns replies in the high-teens to mid-20s percent, while platform-wide cold email reply rates sit in the low single digits — good, well-targeted campaigns reach roughly 5–10%. But a reply rate in isolation is misleading: LinkedIn's higher rate comes off a much smaller, rate-limited base, while email's lower rate runs across a far larger audience.

Where Cold Email Wins

  • Scale and repeatability. Once your sending infrastructure is warmed, you can contact hundreds of prospects a day. LinkedIn simply can't match that ceiling.
  • Cost efficiency. Per contacted prospect, email is dramatically cheaper than a rep's time on LinkedIn — which is what makes it the backbone of most lower-ACV outbound.
  • Predictable pipeline math. With enough volume and stable reply rates, email turns into a forecastable machine — feed in leads, get out meetings. That's the model behind our outbound lead-gen machine.
  • Owning the channel. Your domains and lists are yours; you're not subject to a platform's messaging limits or account restrictions.

The catch: email lives or dies on deliverability. A great message in the spam folder gets 0% replies. Get the fundamentals right first — see our deliverability guide and subject line playbook.

Where LinkedIn Wins

  • Higher reply rates on the right accounts. A personalized note tied to a real signal, delivered in a channel buyers check daily, simply gets answered more often.
  • Warm-feeling first touch. A profile view, a thoughtful comment, then a connection request feels less cold than an unexpected email — useful for senior buyers.
  • Reaching un-emailable people. When you can't find or verify an email, LinkedIn is often the only door.
  • Multi-threading accounts. For enterprise-style deals, LinkedIn makes it natural to engage several stakeholders — the core of our account-based outbound playbook. Agencies specifically can see our LinkedIn outreach for agencies guide.

The catch: LinkedIn does not scale. Connection and message limits, plus the manual effort real personalization requires, cap how many accounts one person can work per week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is cold email or LinkedIn better for B2B in 2026?

Neither is universally better. LinkedIn gets higher reply rates per message (often 15–25%+ when personalized) but is capped on volume; cold email replies in the low single digits but scales to hundreds of contacts a day at a lower cost per prospect. Choose by your list size, deal value, and how much you can personalize — or run both, sequenced, which outperforms either alone.

What are typical reply rates for cold email vs LinkedIn?

Well-targeted cold email typically replies around 3–10%; personalized LinkedIn outreach commonly lands in the high-teens to mid-20s percent. Both vary heavily by industry, seniority, and offer, so benchmark your own numbers rather than chasing averages.

Which is cheaper, cold email or LinkedIn outreach?

Cold email is cheaper per contacted prospect once infrastructure is set up — you're paying for domains, sending tools, and data. LinkedIn costs more in seats, Sales Navigator, and especially rep time, because real personalization doesn't automate well.

Can I just use one channel?

Yes — plenty of teams run email-only for volume or LinkedIn-only for a small set of high-value accounts. But the highest-performing motion in 2026 combines both in a single sequenced cadence triggered by the same buying signal.

Does LinkedIn outreach hurt my account?

It can if you exceed connection/message limits or automate aggressively. Stay within platform limits, keep messages personalized and low-volume per day, and treat LinkedIn as the scalpel — not the volume channel. Use cold email for scale.
