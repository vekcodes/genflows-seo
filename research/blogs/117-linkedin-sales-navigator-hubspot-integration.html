TL;DR

  • HubSpot CRM Sync is an Advanced Plus feature. LinkedIn’s help center says the CRM integration is only available to Sales Navigator Advanced Plus users. Core and Advanced do not include it.
  • It matches and enriches. It is not a lead importer. Sales Navigator leads are matched to existing HubSpot contacts, and creating a contact needs an email address. Bulk “push my saved list into HubSpot” is not documented.
  • Activity write-back is described by LinkedIn but disputed by third parties. Test with one rep before you promise your team that InMails and messages will appear on the HubSpot timeline (directional).
  • Advanced Plus is quote-only. LinkedIn shows Core and Advanced prices but asks you to request a demo for Advanced Plus.
  • For lists, use a compliant data route. Enrich through a data provider or Clay into HubSpot instead of scraping Sales Navigator.

Sales Navigator is where many outbound teams find and filter prospects. HubSpot is where deals, owners and reports live. The question is how the two should connect, and whether the official integration is worth its price.

This guide covers the plan gate, what the sync does, where the documentation goes quiet, and what to do instead when it does not fit. For the Sales Navigator side on its own, see our Sales Navigator outbound setup guide and pricing breakdown.

Sourcing note: plan gating and prices come from LinkedIn’s help center and compare-plans page, read in October 2026. LinkedIn’s long HubSpot enablement guide could not be read in full, so details taken from it are marked (directional). Where nothing is documented, we say so.

The Short Answer

If your team already pays for Advanced Plus and runs HubSpot Sales Hub Professional or higher, turn on CRM Sync. It gives reps LinkedIn context inside HubSpot and helps match leads to existing contacts. If you are on Core or Advanced, the integration is not available to you, and you are better served by a data-provider route into HubSpot. Treat it as a rep-productivity feature, not a lead-sourcing pipeline.

What CRM Sync Does

From LinkedIn’s material, the integration has three jobs:

  • Matching. Sales Navigator leads are matched to HubSpot contacts automatically, using name, contact details and location (directional). If there is no match, a rep can search for one or create a HubSpot contact from Sales Navigator, which needs an email address.
  • Context in both directions. Embedded Profiles put a Sales Navigator panel inside HubSpot. LinkedIn recommends installing it before CRM Sync (directional). Lead IQ in the HubSpot integration needs CRM Sync to be connected.
  • Activity write-back. LinkedIn describes InMails, messages, calls, notes and Smart Link views writing to matched contact records, with account-level write-back limited to notes. Admins switch it on, and each user must authenticate with HubSpot (directional).

Conflicting claims. A competing vendor says the native integration does not import contacts, does not sync automatically, does not log messages or invitations, and does not enrich contacts. LinkedIn’s own guide describes write-back. We could not settle this from documentation. Pilot with one rep and check a test contact’s HubSpot timeline before rolling out.

Setup Notes

  1. Confirm the plan and HubSpot tier before anything else. Wrong plan is the most likely reason you cannot find the option.
  2. Install Embedded Profiles first if you want the panel inside HubSpot (directional). The HubSpot marketplace app is listed as “LinkedIn Data Sync”; we could not verify its tier requirements.
  3. Disconnect HubSpot from Campaign Manager if it is connected, finish the Sales Navigator sync, then reconnect it. LinkedIn suggests testing in a sandbox first (directional).
  4. Have each rep authenticate with HubSpot. Write-back is per user, so a rep who skips this step logs nothing.
  5. Test on one contact. Send a message, then check the HubSpot record.

LinkedIn’s help page links a troubleshooting guide only for Salesforce, not HubSpot. Expect to rely on your LinkedIn account team for HubSpot-specific issues.

What Is Not Documented

  • Field-level mapping and sync frequency.
  • Bulk import of saved leads or lists into HubSpot.
  • Match-rate limits and how duplicates are handled.
  • Whether ownership changes in HubSpot unsave leads in Sales Navigator. LinkedIn’s Dynamics guide says they do not (directional for HubSpot).

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Sales Navigator plan includes the HubSpot integration?

Advanced Plus. LinkedIn’s help center states the CRM integration is only available to Advanced Plus users, so Core and Advanced seats cannot use it.

Which HubSpot plan do I need?

LinkedIn’s enablement guide lists Sales Hub Professional or higher, plus admin rights in both tools (directional; confirm with LinkedIn before buying).

Does Sales Navigator log InMails and messages to HubSpot?

LinkedIn describes write-back of InMails, messages, calls, notes and Smart Link views to matched contacts, but a competing vendor disputes that the integration logs messages (directional). Test with one rep first.

Can I push Sales Navigator leads into HubSpot with it?

Bulk lead import is not documented. The integration matches leads to existing contacts, and creating a contact from Sales Navigator requires an email address.

How much does Advanced Plus cost?

LinkedIn does not publish it; you request a demo. For reference, LinkedIn shows Core at US$119.99 per month and Advanced at US$159.99 per month, as estimates that may exclude tax.

Is it safe to scrape Sales Navigator into HubSpot?

Scraping tools can breach LinkedIn’s terms and risk your account. A data-provider or Clay route into HubSpot avoids touching your Sales Navigator session. Review LinkedIn’s User Agreement for the current rules.
