TL;DR

  • HubSpot is more transparent and faster to launch. It publishes tiers, limits and onboarding fees ($1,500 and $3,500). Salesforce does not publish an implementation price.
  • Salesforce outbound costs more once you add sequences. Pro Suite is $100 per user, and Sales Engagement is a further $50 add-on, so a realistic outbound seat starts around $150, or Core at $195.
  • AI is priced differently. HubSpot meters credits at $0.01 each. Salesforce lists Agentforce for Sales at $125 per user per month.
  • Integrations favour HubSpot. Smartlead lists a native HubSpot connector (directional) but routes Salesforce through a third-party tool.

Most outbound teams pick between these two CRMs when they outgrow a spreadsheet or a starter tool. The question is not which has more features, since Salesforce usually does. It is which one records your replies, runs your follow-up and plays well with your sending stack without a full-time admin.

We have compared Close vs HubSpot, Close vs Salesforce and HubSpot vs Pipedrive, and ranked four options in our best CRM for B2B outbound roundup. This is the direct HubSpot vs Salesforce comparison.

The Short Answer

Choose HubSpot if you are a small or mid-sized outbound team without a dedicated admin and you want to launch quickly, with published pricing and ready connectors to your sending tools. Choose Salesforce if you already run on it, need a complex object or territory model, or have governance requirements that justify the implementation cost. For cold-email-heavy teams either works as the system of record, and high-volume sending should stay in a dedicated tool such as Smartlead or Instantly rather than in CRM sequences.

Pricing: What an Outbound Seat Really Costs

HubSpot Sales Hub

HubSpot lists Free (up to 2 users), Starter at $7 to $20, Professional at $90 to $100 and Enterprise at $150 per seat, with onboarding fees of $1,500 on Professional and $3,500 on Enterprise. Professional is where sequences, automation and reporting get serious for outbound. Full detail is in our HubSpot Sales Hub pricing guide.

Salesforce Sales Cloud

Salesforce's page currently lists Starter Suite at $25, Pro Suite at $100, Core at $195, Advanced at $395 and Max at $550 per user per month, billed annually. Sales Engagement (formerly High Velocity Sales) adds cadences, a work queue and lead scoring for $50 per user per month, and is included in the top editions. No implementation price is published; the page points to partners and Professional Services.

Compare like for like. Pro Suite plus Sales Engagement is about $150 per user, against $90 to $100 for HubSpot Professional (our arithmetic). Add implementation, and the gap usually widens. Salesforce does not publish that cost, so ask for a quote.

Sequences, Sending Limits and Automation

HubSpot Professional allows up to 1,000 one-to-one sequence emails per user per day, and Starter 500. The page lists workflow caps of 50, 300 and 1,000 by tier, and a limit of 5,000 sequences per account. We could not confirm Salesforce's email sending limits or Flow limits by edition, so check them on your quote. In practice, neither CRM is built for high-volume cold email; our HubSpot sequences vs cold email tools post explains why dedicated senders win on mailbox rotation and deliverability.

API Limits and Data Sync

Outbound stacks push a lot of data into the CRM. HubSpot's developer docs list private-app limits of 100 calls per 10 seconds on Free and Starter and 190 on Professional and Enterprise, with daily caps of 250,000, 625,000 and 1,000,000. An API limit add-on raises the burst to 250 and adds 1,000,000 daily calls. Salesforce reportedly starts at 100,000 requests per 24 hours plus an allocation per license (directional), and we could not confirm how that maps to the newer edition names. Either way, batch your syncs. See CRM data hygiene for outbound teams.

AI Features and Cost

HubSpot meters its Breeze AI through HubSpot Credits at $0.01 each, with 500, 3,000 and 5,000 credits included monthly by tier; they reset and do not roll over. Salesforce lists Agentforce for Sales at $125 per user per month, and says its Max edition includes it plus Flex Credits. Per-seat and per-credit models are hard to compare, so estimate usage first. Our Breeze AI guide covers what is worth paying for.

Integrations With Your Outbound Stack

This is where HubSpot has the clearer path. Smartlead advertises a native HubSpot integration covering leads, deals and activities (directional), and routes Salesforce through OutboundSync, a third-party product. Read our guides for Smartlead + HubSpot, Instantly + HubSpot, HeyReach + HubSpot and Clay + HubSpot. We did not find native Salesforce connectors for Instantly, HeyReach or Clay, so verify before you assume one exists.

Admin Overhead and Data Model

Salesforce is the more configurable platform, and that flexibility is a cost for small teams. In our opinion a Salesforce rollout is more likely to need a dedicated admin or partner than HubSpot, though we have no vendor source for a headcount rule. On HubSpot, custom objects are a higher-tier feature; see HubSpot custom objects. We could not confirm object limits by tier for either vendor. Who Should Pick Which

  • Choose HubSpot for teams under roughly 25 reps with no admin, fast launch needs and a Smartlead, Instantly or HeyReach stack.
  • Choose Salesforce if you already run it, need heavy customization or compliance controls, and can fund implementation.
  • Consider neither if you mostly sell by phone and want a simpler tool. See Close vs HubSpot.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does HubSpot Sales Hub cost?

HubSpot lists Starter at $7 to $20 per seat, Professional at $90 to $100 and Enterprise at $150. Professional adds a $1,500 onboarding fee and Enterprise $3,500. See our Sales Hub pricing guide for which figure is monthly and which is annual.

Does Salesforce include sequences?

Not in the entry editions as far as we could confirm. Salesforce sells Sales Engagement, formerly High Velocity Sales, as a $50 per user per month add-on, and lists it as included in its top editions. Check which edition your quote includes.

What are HubSpot's email sending limits?

HubSpot lists 500 one-to-one emails per user per day on Starter and 1,000 on Professional. We could not confirm a Salesforce equivalent. For high-volume cold email, most teams send from a dedicated tool instead. See HubSpot email sending limits.

How do the AI prices compare?

HubSpot charges $0.01 per HubSpot Credit, with 500 to 5,000 credits included monthly by tier. Salesforce lists Agentforce for Sales at $125 per user per month, and says its Max edition includes it. The two are priced on different models, so compare against real usage.

Do they connect to Smartlead, Instantly and HeyReach?

HubSpot has the clearer path: Smartlead lists a native HubSpot integration (directional), and see our guides for Instantly and HeyReach. For Salesforce, Smartlead points to a third-party tool, and we did not find native connectors for the others.

Will I need a Salesforce admin?

In our opinion, yes more often than with HubSpot. Salesforce setup and ownership are generally heavier, but we have no vendor source for a headcount rule. A small team with no admin will usually move faster on HubSpot. If you are switching, see our migration guide.
