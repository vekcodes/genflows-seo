Dripify is a cloud LinkedIn automation tool that competes with HeyReach, Waalaxy and Expandi. Its pricing looks simple, but the details that matter for a team are seats, which features sit on which plan, and what the terms say when you want out. This guide lays them out.

Prices and plan features were read from Dripify's pricing, team-management and terms pages in October 2026. Vendor claims are labelled as such and some table markers are (directional); confirm on the live page before you buy.

The Short Answer

Dripify costs $59 to $79 per seat per month for Pro and $79 to $99 for Advanced, depending on annual or monthly billing. Pick Pro if each rep runs their own sequences and you need HubSpot, webhooks or Zapier. Pick Advanced if a manager needs roles, team analytics or A/B testing. Because every seat is one LinkedIn account, an agency managing many client profiles will find per-seat pricing adds up fast.

What Advanced Adds

  • Team management with Owner, Manager and User roles, team splits, lead deduplication across teammates and shared templates.
  • A/B testing, step analytics, lead tagging and evergreen leads.
  • Activity control, LinkedIn lead transfer and team analytics.

Pro seats can still join a team and use shared templates and deduplication, but Manager and Owner roles need Advanced. We did not verify how a mixed Pro and Advanced team is billed.

Add-Ons: Email Finder Credits

Pro and Advanced each include 100 email-finder credits. Extra credits are sold in packs and, per Dripify, expire after 30 days, do not roll over, and are charged only on successful lookups.

Daily Limits and Safety Claims

Dripify's pricing page lists daily quotas of 75 connection requests, 100+ messages, 30 Sales Navigator InMails and 200+ profile views, identical on Pro and Advanced, and says you set your own volumes. It also markets human behaviour simulation, local IP access and cloud-based performance.

These are vendor claims, not guarantees. Dripify's terms state it is not produced, endorsed or certified by LinkedIn and include no ban-risk guarantee. Keep real volumes well under the quotas and read our limits and account safety guide; if a profile is restricted, see the recovery playbook.

Free Trial, Cancellation and Refunds

  • Trial: 7 days, no credit card, Advanced features. Access is restricted afterward unless you pick a plan.
  • Cancel: any time from the Billing tab or by emailing support, effective at the end of the paid term. Subscriptions auto-renew.
  • Refunds: monthly purchases are non-refundable; annual plans only within 24 hours of payment.
  • Changes: upgrades apply immediately and downgrades from the next cycle. Removing a team member does not cancel their billing.

Who Dripify Fits

Good fit: teams of 1 to 10 reps who each run their own LinkedIn account, want cloud automation at a simple per-seat price, and need HubSpot, Zapier or webhooks without the top plan.

Weaker fit: agencies running many client accounts (the Agency program has no public details), buyers who need Pipedrive or Close native sync (not found on the pages we read), and anyone who wants a refund safety net on monthly billing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Dripify cost in 2026?

Pro is $79 per seat per month, or $59 on annual billing. Advanced is $99 monthly or $79 annually, and Enterprise is custom and based on seat count. The older Basic tier no longer appears on the pricing page.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: 7 days, no credit card, with access to Advanced features. Nothing is charged automatically at the end. You choose a plan or the account goes inactive.

Do I pay per user or per LinkedIn account?

Both, because each seat connects one LinkedIn account. A five-rep team needs five seats, so cost scales linearly with headcount. Teams of 10 or more seats are directed to sales.

What are Dripify's daily limits?

The pricing page lists 75 connection requests, 100+ messages, 30 Sales Navigator InMails and 200+ profile views per day, and says you set your own volumes within those ranges. These are vendor-stated quotas, not a safety guarantee; see LinkedIn outreach limits and account safety.

Does Dripify integrate with HubSpot, Zapier and webhooks?

HubSpot is listed from Pro up, and webhooks and Zapier are listed on both plans. We did not find Pipedrive or Close native integrations on the pages we read, so test those before you buy. For the wider data flow, see syncing cold email and LinkedIn data into HubSpot.

Can I get a refund or cancel anytime?

You can cancel anytime from the Billing tab, effective at the end of the paid term, and subscriptions auto-renew. Per Dripify's terms, monthly purchases are non-refundable and annual plans are refundable only within 24 hours of payment.
