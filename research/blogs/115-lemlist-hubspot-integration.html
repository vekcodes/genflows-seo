TL;DR

  • lemlist has a native, bi-directional HubSpot integration. A HubSpot admin connects it with OAuth, and you choose the sync direction field by field.
  • Sends and replies log to HubSpot as Email objects. Opens, clicks, bounces and “interested” markers log as Notes, because HubSpot does not let third-party apps store them as emails.
  • Only one lemlist team can connect to one HubSpot account. Plan your workspace structure before you link.
  • The native docs do not describe reply-triggered deals, tasks or lifecycle changes. For that, use lemlist webhooks into n8n, Make, Zapier or a HubSpot workflow.
  • Manual unsubscribes do not sync to HubSpot. Only self-unsubscribes do, so set your own suppression property.

lemlist sends the emails and LinkedIn touches. HubSpot is where your reps, reports and attribution live. If a reply sits in lemlist and never becomes a task or a deal in HubSpot, the pipeline looks emptier than it is and good leads go cold.

This guide covers what the native integration does, what lands in HubSpot as what, where the documentation stops, and how to close the gaps. The Short Answer

Use the native integration for what it does well: importing HubSpot lists into lemlist, mapping fields, and logging email and LinkedIn activity on the contact timeline. Add a webhook flow only for the actions it does not document, such as creating a deal or task when a lead replies or is marked interested.

How to Set It Up

  1. Connect with OAuth. In lemlist, go to Settings > Integrations and start the HubSpot connection. lemlist says it does not store your HubSpot credentials. A HubSpot admin has to authorize it.
  2. Map users. Each lemlist sender maps to a HubSpot user, manually or by matching email. Several lemlist users can map to one HubSpot user, but not the other way round. Tasks and ownership depend on this step.
  3. Choose sync direction per field. Options are HubSpot priority, lemlist priority, bi-sync or deactivate. lemlist recommends HubSpot as the source of truth, and so do we.
  4. Set mandatory properties and defaults. Mandatory properties block contact or company creation until filled. Default values apply to the Contact, Company or Deal records lemlist creates.
  5. Import your first list. Pull from an active HubSpot list or contact view. Contacts that existed before you linked must be imported manually.
  6. Pick which activities sync, then test with one contact before you launch a campaign.

Two setup traps. Field mapping is not retroactive: records imported and campaigns launched before you map a field will not backfill. And imports only pull fields that are visible as columns in the HubSpot list view, so add the columns first.

CallsCall

Activities attach only to a HubSpot contact with a matching email address. Calls need the contact to exist first; otherwise the call waits and syncs once the contact appears. Call notes are capped at 65,000 characters, with the transcript cut first, and are overwritten on each refresh, so edits made in HubSpot are lost.

Why this matters for reporting

Because opens and clicks are Notes, they will not show in HubSpot’s email reporting or sequence metrics. If your dashboard counts “emails sent” or “replies,” those come through; engagement does not. Build engagement reporting from lemlist, or log it as a property. See our RevOps dashboard guide for what to report where.

Duplicates and Sync Speed

lemlist checks existing contacts by email and existing companies by domain or name before creating records. Its troubleshooting guide adds that matching can use email, LinkedIn URL or company domain, and that contacts without a valid identifier may not match. Merge and conflict behaviour beyond that is not documented, so keep your own hygiene rules; see CRM data hygiene for outbound teams.

lemlist says sync typically takes 2 to 10 minutes, can lag under heavy volume, and suggests waiting up to 30 minutes at scale before contacting support after an hour. Errors show in Settings > Integrations > HubSpot > Logs. Two you will see: CONTACT_FIND_FIRST (the contact must exist in HubSpot, so enable creation or add it) and a deal-not-found error when a synced deal does not exist in lemlist.

lemlist webhooks send real-time callbacks for events such as replies and interested markers; check the live event list in lemlist’s developer docs because the grouped event types are described differently across sources (directional). If you build your own flow, HubSpot’s documented limits apply to your app: a burst limit per 10 seconds (100 on Free and Starter, 190 on Professional and Enterprise) and a daily cap (250,000, 625,000 and 1,000,000 respectively) (directional: read from a summary of HubSpot’s usage guidelines; confirm on the page). Exceeding them returns 429 errors. Use a search-then-upsert pattern keyed on email, and compare tools in our n8n vs Make vs Zapier guide.

A Reply-to-Deal Pattern That Works

  1. lemlist fires a webhook when a lead replies or is marked interested.
  2. Your flow searches HubSpot by email. If the contact is missing, it creates one.
  3. It sets a property such as outbound_status = Interested and assigns the owner.
  4. A HubSpot workflow on that property creates the deal, the task and the Slack alert. We cover the workflow side in reply handling with HubSpot workflows.
  5. Routing and speed matter more than the tool: see lead routing and speed to lead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect lemlist to HubSpot?

A HubSpot admin authorizes the connection with OAuth in lemlist under Settings > Integrations, then a lemlist admin configures user and field mapping. One HubSpot account can link to only one lemlist team.

Does the lemlist HubSpot integration sync both ways?

Yes. You set the direction for each field: HubSpot priority, lemlist priority, bi-sync or off. lemlist recommends treating HubSpot as the source of truth.

What shows up in HubSpot from lemlist?

Sent emails and replies appear as Emails, and LinkedIn messages as LinkedIn Messages. Opens, clicks, bounces and interested markers appear as Notes, and calls sync as Calls.

Why are opens and clicks logged as Notes?

lemlist says HubSpot does not let third-party apps store opens and clicks as Email objects, so they are logged as Notes on the contact.

Does a reply create a deal or update the lifecycle stage?

The native docs we read do not describe it, though deal creation can be set per campaign. For reply-triggered deals, tasks or lifecycle changes, use lemlist webhooks with an automation tool or a HubSpot workflow.

Are unsubscribes and bounces synced to HubSpot?

Self-unsubscribes sync as Notes and bounces are logged as Notes. Manual additions to lemlist’s Unsubscribe section are not pushed to the CRM, so set your own opt-out or suppression property.
