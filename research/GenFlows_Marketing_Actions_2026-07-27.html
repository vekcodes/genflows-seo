P0 — this week

M1 · Connect Google Search Console P0

Still not connected — the OAuth token was never minted, so there is no impression, click, position or indexation data available to work from. This is the single biggest blocker on the analysis side and it takes five minutes.
Do this Run python3 research/gsc_connect.py and authorise as bivek@genflowsagency.com. If the script lists zero properties, first verify genflows.com in Search Console and grant that Google account at least Full User access.
Unlocks Striking-distance queries (positions 5–20 — the fastest wins available), CTR gaps, query-to-page mapping, real indexation status, and the Links report that finally quantifies our backlink position instead of inferring it.

P1 — next two to four weeks

M3 · Grant the HubSpot private-app token the files scope P1

The token can edit posts but cannot manage files, so featured images cannot be uploaded or attached programmatically.
Do this HubSpot → Settings → Integrations → Private Apps → the SEO automation app → Scopes → add files and files.ui_hidden.read. The token string does not change, so nothing needs re-configuring afterwards.
Unblocks M4.

M4 · Featured images for 67 posts P1

Confirmed via the API: 67 of 71 posts have no featured image, which is why og:image is empty and every share renders blank. The developer is adding a branded fallback (technical item D5) so no share is ever blank again — but a fallback is a safety net, not a substitute for real per-post creative.
Do this Decide the creative approach, then the automation can bulk-attach once M3 is done:
  1. Templated (recommended to start): one 1200×630 template — post title on the ink background with the flame accent and logo — generated per post. Cheap, consistent, instantly covers all 67.
  2. Bespoke for the ~10 highest-intent commercial posts (software comparisons, pricing posts), templated for the rest.
Also supply One branded default 1200×630 PNG for the developer's fallback (D5), and alt text conventions for the generated images.

M5 · Backlink / digital-PR programme P1 — the real lever

We are targeting commercial head terms ("best cold email software", "clay pricing", "best AI SDR tools") where incumbents have years of domain age and hundreds of referring domains. On-page quality does not beat that; links do. Everything in the technical document makes the site correct and fast — none of it substitutes for authority.
Do this
  • Partner links first — the cheapest wins we have. We already display Clay Artisan Solutions Partner, Smartlead Certified Partner and First 100 Partner badges. Ask each for a directory listing or partner-page link, and for a co-written case study.
  • Client case studies with the logos already on the homepage (UDS, Emergent3, Symbus, Tweezr, Flizz) — ask each for a link from their site or a joint post.
  • Guest posts and expert quotes in GTM/RevOps publications and newsletters; the 71-post library is the credibility proof.
  • Directory listings: Clutch, G2 (agency category), relevant HubSpot/Clay/Smartlead solution directories.
Measure Referring-domain count monthly, from the GSC Links report once M1 is done.

M6 · Off-site AEO presence P1

AI answer engines weight what other sites say about us. Our on-site AEO is now strong — answer-first TL;DRs, comparison tables, FAQ schema live on 54 posts — but with zero third-party mentions, there is little for a model to cite us from.
Do this Build genuine presence where these engines read: relevant subreddits (r/sales, r/SaaS, r/RevOps) answering real questions without link-dropping; G2/Clutch reviews and an agency profile; consistent LinkedIn posting from the founder account tied to blog topics; getting listed in third-party "best cold email agency" and "Clay experts" roundups.
Measure Query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews monthly for our target questions and log whether GenFlows is cited. That log is the AEO scoreboard.

M7 · Striking-distance optimisation P1 — needs M1

What Pull every query where we rank 5–20, group by landing page, and edit the page to match the intent that is already earning impressions. This is the highest-ROI recurring SEO work that exists, and it is impossible without Search Console.
Do this Once M1 is live, run the pull with research/gsc_actions.py, then work the list weekly: strongest impressions with worst CTR first.

M8 · Author identity P1

Every post is attributed to "GenFlows Team" and links to /blog/author/genflows-team, while a real author page exists at /consultancy/wouter. Search engines and AI engines both reward content attributed to an identifiable, credentialled human.
Do this — content side Supply the author bio (2–3 sentences, credentials, years in GTM), a headshot, and the personal LinkedIn URL. Then create the HubSpot blog author record and set it as the author on the posts. The developer wires it into schema and the byline (technical item D8).

M12 · Decide what /v3 and /v5 are for P1

Both are live, indexable and in the sitemap. /v3 is an alternate homepage ("Cold email works. We make it work for you.") competing with / for the same terms. /v5 is an MCA/SBA/commercial-finance variant. The developer is noindexing both as the safe default (technical item D6) — but the decision is yours.
Answer one question Is either page meant to earn organic traffic? If yes it needs its own keyword target and its own copy, not homepage messaging. If it is a paid-traffic landing page, noindex is correct and permanent. If it is a retired experiment, say so and it gets unpublished with a redirect to /.

M10 · The 17 posts with no FAQ section P2

FAQ schema is live on 54 of 71 posts. The 17 without it are the older June batches, which were written before the house format included an FAQ block — there is nothing to mark up, so this is a content gap rather than a markup gap.
Do this Add a six-question FAQ section to each in the house format, then re-run the schema injection so all 71 posts carry FAQPage. Prioritise the commercial ones (gtm-engineering-tools, clay-workflow-automation, cold-email-infrastructure-deliverability, gtm-engineering-agency-how-to-choose).

M11 · Cannibalisation review P2 — needs M1

We have four deliverability posts and five LinkedIn posts. They are cross-linked to disambiguate, but only query data proves whether two of them are competing for the same term. Once GSC is connected, check for pages swapping positions on one query and either consolidate or re-angle the weaker one.

M13 · Note the analytics correction date P2

The site currently loads the GA4 tag twice, so every page view has been counted twice — sessions are inflated and bounce/conversion rates understated. The developer is removing the duplicate (technical item D9).
Do this Record the fix date as an annotation in GA4 and in any reporting. Traffic will appear to halve on that date; that is the measurement being corrected, not a real decline. Do not compare pre- and post-fix periods without noting it.

What to realistically expect

  • Weeks 1–4: the nine missing posts get indexed; FAQ rich results and first AI citations become possible; social and AI cards stop rendering blank once images land.
  • Months 2–3: long-tail and low-competition queries start ranking. Striking-distance edits from GSC produce the first page-1 wins. Median post age is still under three months, so this is the normal curve, not underperformance.
  • Months 3–6+: head terms move only if M5 and M6 are genuinely underway. Without authority, on-page work caps out — that ceiling is the honest constraint on the whole programme.
