0 · Executive summary — the honest verdict

Nothing is fundamentally broken. The single biggest reason we're not ranking is that the content is brand new on a low-authority domain. 50 of 62 posts are under 30 days old (median 18 days). New pages on a young domain routinely take 3–6 months to rank for competitive terms; AI answer engines lag further still. We are in the normal "waiting" window — not a penalty.

That said, the audit surfaced real, fixable problems slowing us down and a set of AEO (AI-search) opportunities we're leaving entirely on the table:

  1. 18 of 62 posts — every high-value commercial post from the last 6 batches — are missing from the sitemap. Google may not know they exist yet.
  2. Zero FAQ schema, despite every post having a 6-question FAQ section. Biggest single AI-search / rich-result miss.
  3. No off-page authority (backlinks) — the real reason competitive terms won't move regardless of on-page quality.
  4. Secondary issues: one post stuck on a 1970 publish date; sitemap not declared in robots.txt; 58/62 posts have no featured image; 52/62 title tags too long.
  5. GSC still not connected — we're blind on impressions/positions and "striking distance" wins.

The content itself is strong and unusually AEO-ready (long-form, answer-first TL;DRs, comparison tables, cited sources, clean H-structure). The gap is discovery, authority, and machine-readable markup — not quality.

Median age 18 days. Google needs time to crawl, index, gather engagement signals and assess trust — typically a quarter+ on a domain without established authority. Expected and self-resolving with time + the fixes below.

1.2 Domain authority & backlinks — the real competitive lever

We target commercial, high-competition keywords ("best cold email software", "clay pricing", "cold email cost", "best CRM for outbound") held by aged, heavily-linked incumbents (growthtoday.co, RevPartners, vendor domains). On-page quality alone does not beat that — links do. genflows.com is a young domain with very few referring domains. (Exact count needs GSC "Links" or Ahrefs/Semrush — flagged, not yet measured.)

1.3 Discovery gap — 18 posts missing from the sitemap

Our newest, most valuable posts aren't in /sitemap.xml (§2.1). If Google can't find a URL, it can't rank it — compounding 1.1 on exactly the pages we most want ranking.

1.4 We can't optimize what we can't measure (GSC)

Without Search Console we have no visibility into impressions, average position, or "striking-distance" queries (positions 5–20, where small edits produce fast wins). Connecting GSC is a prerequisite, not a nice-to-have.

4 · AEO / AI-search readiness — the biggest opportunity

AEO = getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude. Our content is structurally well-suited; we're missing the markup that makes extraction trivial.

4.1 No FAQPage schema — the #1 AEO miss

Every post has a 6-Q&A FAQ section, but none are marked up as FAQPage (confirmed on 3 sampled posts). FAQ schema drives Google FAQ rich results & People-Also-Ask, and lets AI engines lift our Q&As verbatim as cited answers. High impact, low effort — the Q&As already exist. (Caveat: HubSpot may strip <script> from post body; reliable path is a theme/template FAQ module or a post-level HTML-injection field — verify before rollout.)

4.2 What already helps AEO

  • Answer-first TL;DR boxes — ideal for AI extraction.
  • Comparison tables (pricing/tools/thresholds) — AI engines favour structured comparisons.
  • Cited sources & dates — supports trust / E-E-A-T.
  • Baseline BlogPosting / Organization / Person / BreadcrumbList JSON-LD present.

4.3 AEO gaps beyond FAQ schema

  • Author E-E-A-T: add author with real bio + link to author page (/consultancy/wouter exists — wire into schema + bylines).
  • Entity clarity: add Organization sameAs (LinkedIn, Clay/Smartlead partner badges we already display).
  • Off-site citations: AI engines weight Reddit / LinkedIn / G2 / comparison-site mentions. Zero presence = zero AI citations regardless of on-site quality.
  • Publish llms.txt — emerging convention some AI crawlers read.

5 · Content-strategy findings

  • Breadth is excellent — 62 posts across 5 clusters (Clay/GTM, Cold Email, Outbound, RevOps, Growth), each mapped to a service line. A real asset once authority arrives.
  • Cannibalization watch — many close cold-email posts (deliverability ×4, LinkedIn ×5). Cross-linked to disambiguate; confirm with GSC that two posts aren't competing for one query.
  • No explicit pillar pages — designate one pillar per cluster with hub-and-spoke linking to concentrate authority.
  • No conversion path — posts end with a CTA to /; build dedicated service landing pages per cluster.
